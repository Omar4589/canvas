A campaign goes from "created" to "canvassers in the field" through one ordered chain. Your campaign dashboard has a **Setup progress** card that walks you through it with a highlighted next step, so you never have to remember the order.

## The setup chain

1. **Create the campaign** — go to **Campaigns → New campaign**. Give it a name, type (Survey or Lit drop), and state. A survey isn't required yet.
2. **Import voters** — on **Voter Import**, upload your voter file. New addresses land in **Intake**, owned by no walk list yet.
3. **Attach a survey** *(survey campaigns only)* — on the campaign's **Survey** tab, pick or build the survey. You can do this any time before you activate a pass.
4. **Create a walk list and give it doors** — on **Walk Lists**, make a walk list, then **Claim** its doors (from a saved search, or "Claim all Intake"). This auto-creates its **Pass 1**.
5. **Cut and accept books** — on **Turf Cutting**, cut the pass's doors into walkable books, then **Accept** them.
6. **Assign canvassers** — assign books to people. Add canvassers on **Users** first if you have none.
7. **Activate the pass** — open the walk list's **Passes** panel and **Activate**. Now it's live and the field app shows the work.

> Tip: You can't activate a pass until it has at least one **accepted** book (and, for survey campaigns, a survey attached). The dashboard nudges you toward whatever's missing.

## What to do next

Once you're live, the dashboard becomes your monitoring home — knocks, surveys, and coverage roll in as your team works. See [Get started as a canvasser](canvasser-first-day) for what your field team sees on their phones.
