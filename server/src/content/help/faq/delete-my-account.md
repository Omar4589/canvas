Open the mobile app and go to **Profile ▸ Delete account**.

Deleting is **permanent**. It removes your login, your name, your email address, your phone number and your
password, and **no admin can bring the account back**. That's different from an admin *deactivating* you,
which can be undone at any time.

## What stays with the campaign

The doors you knocked and the survey answers you recorded **stay with the campaign**. Those are the
organization's records of work performed — not your personal content — so deleting your account never
changes a campaign's numbers. On reports and on the map you'll simply appear as **"Deleted user"** from
then on.

Your name is also kept alongside those records for **180 days**, so your organization can still verify who
did which field work, and then it's removed for good — the records stay with the campaign, but they no
longer directly identify you. Canvassing records include the location where each door was logged, and an
organization has to be able to check that work against a real person. You'll see this explained in the app
before you confirm.

## Things that will stop the deletion

The app tells you which one applies:

- **You have doors that haven't synced yet.** If you knocked houses while offline and they haven't reached
  the server, the app syncs them first and won't delete until it's done — otherwise that work would be
  thrown away.
- **You're the only admin of your organization.** Deleting would leave nobody who can add users, cut turf,
  or run reports. Make someone else an admin first.
- **You're the only admin who can manage billing.** With no bill-payer left, nobody could pay the
  subscription and the whole organization would eventually go read-only.

## What happens to your walk lists

Any books or walk lists assigned to you are **handed back** and become unassigned, so no doors are left
stranded. You come off every campaign roster.

## If you come back later

Deleting frees up your email address, so an admin can add you again. You'll get a **brand-new account** —
your old knocks stay attached to the deleted one.

**Already uninstalled the app?** You can request deletion at **doorline.app/delete-account**.
