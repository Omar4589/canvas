With a **tag** — it's the one number in the app that counts supporters as *people*.

Answer counts count **answers**: survey Maria in two rounds and her two "Support" answers count twice — correct for "how many conversations went well," wrong for "how many supporters do we have." A tag counts each voter **once**, however many rounds or questions they matched in.

## Set it up (about a minute)

1. Open your survey in the builder.
2. On the **Support** option, click **+ tag** and pick (or ask an admin to create) a tag — say, **Supporter**.
3. Tag **Likely Support** with the same tag if you count leaners. Tag "Yes" on a yard-sign question too, if wanting a sign means support to you — tags roll up across questions.

That's it — and it's **retroactive**: the moment an option carries the tag, every answer ever recorded for it counts. No re-surveying, no waiting.

## Read the two numbers

Open the campaign Home's survey results (or the Survey Explorer). The **Tags** panel shows, per tag:

- **Voters** (identified) — everyone who **ever** gave a tagged answer, each person once. This number never goes down.
- **Current** — how many people's **most recent answer** still carries the tag. Maria says Support in round 1 and Opposed in round 2? She stays identified, but drops out of current. **This is the number for "how many supporters do we have now."** (A later visit that skipped the question doesn't change anything — the last answer they actually gave still stands.)

## Split it by team

Click the tag and you get a **By team** table: each crew's identified and still-current voters. Credit goes to the **first team to tag the voter** — if Team B re-knocks someone Team A found, Team A keeps them — so the team rows always **add up exactly** to the campaign total. There's deliberately no per-canvasser version: several questions can feed one voter's tag, so "who recorded it" has no single answer.

## Use it

- **Re-canvass or phone-bank your supporters:** build a [saved search](saved-searches) with the **By tag** filter and export the CSV.
- **Show it to your candidate:** in the [client report builder](client-reports), tick the tag under **Visible tags** — the published report gets a "Supporter — 400 identified · 380 still current" row. Unticked tags never appear.
- **See one round at a time:** the pass picker scopes both numbers to that round. Note that "current" then means "as of that round" — the all-rounds view is the one that answers "supporters today."
