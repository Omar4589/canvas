Yes. A client link opens either **all your reports** for the campaign or **just one report**.

- **All your reports** is the usual choice: one link and one password, and next week's report shows up on it too.
- **Just this report** is for someone who should see only this one, like a donor or a board member. It has its own password, and next week's report won't be on it.

## Making one

On a report your client can see (published, not taken down), press **Share**, on its row or at the top of the report. Press **Make a link**, choose **Just this report**, give it a label only you see (say "Board"), pick how long it lasts, and press **Create link**. Its password is shown once, right there, with **Copy password**: copy it then, and send it separately from the message. The message in **Share** then uses that link and ends "— I'll send you its password separately."

You can also make one from the **Client links** panel: **+ New link**, then **Opens: One report only** and pick the report. Only reports your client can see now are offered.

## What the person sees

The link opens straight on that report. There's no list of other reports, no "‹ All reports" and no Older or Newer, and the page says "This link opens this report only." If that report's earlier totals changed after it was sent, its note doesn't name any other report: "Totals from before Oct 19 were updated by −120 doors after they were first reported."

## Later

- In the **Client links** panel, links to one report are folded under **Links to one report**, each saying which report it opens: "Opens only the Oct 19 – Oct 25 report (Whole campaign)".
- **Take down** the report and the link shows "This report was taken down or removed. Ask the person who sent it." until you put it back up. The Take down confirm names the link, and the **Client links** panel says "Its report is taken down — the link shows “taken down” until you put it back up."
- **Delete** the report and its one-report links are deleted with it, for good: the link then says "This link isn't available. It may have been replaced or switched off — ask the person who sent it." The Delete confirm names them, and History records each one as "Deleted with its report".
