So your client always gets exactly the report you meant to send. **Each published report has one link of its own**, and it opens only that report: no list of other reports, no other weeks, and next week's report never appears on it. Make a report, share its link; next week, make the next one and share its link. For the totals so far, make a **Latest progress** report and share that.

## Making a report's link

On a published report, press **Share**, on its row or at the top of the report. The first time, it makes the link: pick the password and how long the link lasts, then press **Create link**.

- **Same as your last link** is picked for you when this tab already has a link whose password Doorline kept, so your client keeps **one password for the season** and you send a password only the first time.
- **Make one up for me**, or **Type my own** (12 or more characters).

After that, **Share** on that report always shows the same link, with its password (press **Show**) and a message to paste into your email or text. There's never a second link to choose between.

## What your client sees

The link opens straight on its report, and the page says "This link opens this report only." If you take the report down, the link says "This report was taken down or removed. Ask the person who sent it." until you put it back up. If you delete the report, its link is deleted with it, and says "This link isn't available. It may have been replaced or switched off — ask the person who sent it."

## Someone else should see one report

A donor or a board member who should see one report gets **the same link and password as your client**: a report can't have a second link. If you later need to cut that person off, press **Change password** in **Share** on that report. It offers to change the password on your other links that use the same one too: leave that ticked and your client needs the new password for all of them; untick it and only this report's link changes.

## Links from before

- A report that got two links before each report had its own keeps both, and both keep working. **Share** shows the second one under the first ("Also opens this report: Board · made Oct 25") with **Delete**.
- An old link to all your reports opens every report until Doorline turns those links off. See [Where did the Client links panel go?](where-did-client-links-go)
