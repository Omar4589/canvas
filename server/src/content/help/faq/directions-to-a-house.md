Tap **Directions →** under the address. You'll find it on the house screen, on the map's pull-up panel when you tap a pin, and on the building screen for apartments.

Pick the app you want and it opens with walking directions:

- **On an iPhone:** Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze.
- **On Android:** Google Maps, or **Another maps app** to use whatever else you have installed.

If the app you pick isn't on your phone, the directions open in your browser instead, so the tap never leads nowhere.

## What if the pin is in the wrong place?

Directions still take you to the right house. Doorline hands your maps app the **street address**, not the pin you see on the map, so your maps app looks the address up on its own. A pin sitting on the neighbour's lot doesn't send you to the neighbour's door.

That's also a good way to check a suspicious pin. If your maps app puts the address somewhere clearly different from where Doorline's pin sits, tell your team lead — they can move the pin, and everyone picks up the corrected spot on their next sync. See [Your field map](canvasser-map).

Knock the door you actually stood at either way.

## Does tapping it record anything?

No. Directions is just a hand-off to your maps app. Nothing is recorded, no door status changes, and tapping it doesn't send your location anywhere.

Doorline collects your location only at the moment you record a door. The blue dot that follows you on the map is drawn on your own phone to help you navigate, and it isn't sent to us unless you record something. See [Why does Doorline need my location?](why-location-required).

Which app opens is up to your phone. Android remembers the maps app you normally use, and you can change that in your phone's settings.
