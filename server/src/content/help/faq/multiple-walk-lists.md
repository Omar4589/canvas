Yes. A campaign can have **many active walk lists** running at the same time — split by area (North / South) or by team (paid vs. volunteers). Each walk list owns its own disjoint doors, its own crew, and its own active pass, so they never step on each other.

Canvassers only ever see the books assigned to them. A canvasser who's on more than one walk list gets a small switcher on their Books screen to pick which one they're working.

See [Walk lists](walk-lists).
