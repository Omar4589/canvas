**Nothing is wrong.** For security, Doorline asks for your password again from time to time. A screen
covers the app or the console page — **"Enter your password to keep going"** — with your email
already filled in. Type your password and you're back exactly where you were: the same map or list on
the phone, the same page and unsaved edits on the web. Nothing is deleted while it waits.

**When it asks.** Using Doorline keeps you signed in — opening the app or using the console quietly
extends your sign-in about once a day, once your app is up to date. It asks again after **30 days
without using Doorline**, or **90 days after you last typed your password**, however often you use it.
(Doorline staff accounts: 30 days from sign-in, never extended.)

**In the field app:**

- **It never interrupts a door.** If it comes up while you're at a door or in a survey, the app lets
  you finish — anything you save is kept on the phone — and asks once you're back on the map or your
  list.
- **No signal to sign in?** After the sign-in fails for lack of signal, tap **Later** and keep
  knocking. The map shows **"Password needed"** with how many items are waiting; your team can't see
  them until you enter your password, so do it as soon as you have signal.
- **Forgot your password?** Tap **Forgot password?** on that screen, set a new one in the browser, come
  back and type it in. Back within half an hour of tapping it, your houses are still there; once it asks
  for your new password, type it within half an hour. After that the app signs out to be safe: anything
  you recorded stays on the phone and is sent once you sign in with the new password.

**Sometimes you really are signed out** — when your password was changed on another device, or the
account was deleted. Then you land on the sign-in screen with a note saying why. Signing out in one
browser tab signs out your other tabs too, the next time they reach Doorline — except one already
showing the password screen, which waits for you.

**Always sign back in as yourself.** Anything saved on the phone goes up under whoever signs in next.
Doorline can still turn an item down — for example if you were taken off the campaign while signed
out — and those items aren't kept. The one other way to lose saved work is deleting the app (or
clearing its data) while the pending badge on **Refresh** shows a number, so don't.

**Lost a phone that was signed in, or left a shared computer signed in?** Change your password yourself
— **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page, or your profile. Every other device then needs the new
password: the next time it reaches Doorline it signs out, and a phone deletes its saved houses as it
does. The one exception is a device whose password screen came up or was used in the half hour before
(that's what tapping **Forgot password?** there and coming straight back looks like): it asks for the
new password instead, until it has been left untouched for half an hour. A phone with no signal keeps
working on its saved houses until it connects. A temporary password from your admin or team lead doesn't
sign other devices out.

**Your app hasn't updated yet?** It sends you to the sign-in screen when your sign-in runs out (instead
of the password screen above) and, like any sign-out, clears the houses saved on the phone. Sign in with
your usual email and password when you have signal, and they download again. See
[The app asks to restart](app-asks-to-restart) and
[The app says an update is available](app-says-update-available) for getting the newest version.
