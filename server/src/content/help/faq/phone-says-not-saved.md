After the app update, the map says **"1 item wasn't saved — tap to see why"** when something you saved without signal reached Doorline but couldn't be accepted any more. You might also see it on Books, in the menu, on the organization picker, or on the screen that says you're not on this campaign.

**Why it happens.** Your phone sends what you saved once it has signal. If things changed in the meantime, Doorline can't take it. For example:

- you were taken off the campaign, or out of the organization;
- the walk list's survey was changed to a different one;
- the voter asked not to be contacted;
- the house or the campaign was removed.

**What to do.** Tap the notice. For each item you'll see the door (when your phone still has your books), what you recorded, when, and the reason. Nothing is sent again from the phone, so **knock the door again if you can**, or tell your team lead. Your team sees these on the console's Audit page too. Tap **Remove** to clear one. They clear themselves after 30 days.

Only you see your list. Anyone else who signs in on the phone doesn't.

## Other lines about saved items

- **"Items not uploaded yet"** when you sign out means some of your saves haven't gone up yet. They stay on the phone and upload the next time **you** sign in there. If you can, wait for signal before you sign out.
- **"3 saved items from Jane D. are waiting on this phone"** on the sign-in screen or in the menu means someone else's saves are still on this phone. They upload only when that person signs in here, never under your name. If nobody signs in for them, they're removed after 30 days.
- **"… from before the app update will upload under the next person who signs in"** is about saves made on the older app. They don't know who recorded them, so the first person to sign in after the update uploads them. Make sure that's the person who recorded them.
- **"… from a deleted account were removed from this phone"** means the account that saved them was deleted, so they couldn't be credited to anyone.
