When one client wants separate reports for, say, a **volunteer team** and a **paid team**, you make a **team** for each and give each its own reports. They sit in their own tab on your **Client Reports** page, next to the whole campaign, and each report has its own link to send.

**A team is a name your client sees, plus one or more crews.** A crew is a crew lead (coordinator) and the canvassers who report to them on this campaign. "Paid team" can be Sam's crew and Dee's crew together; "Volunteer team" can be Maria's crew alone.

## Making one

On the campaign's **Client Reports** tab, press **New report**, choose **A team**, then **+ New team**. Type the name your client will see and tick its crews. Then pick the dates and press **Create draft**.

- **The name can't include a canvasser's name**, because your client reads it. "Paid team" is fine; "Dee's team" is refused.
- **A crew can be in only one team.** The list says when a crew already belongs to another team.
- **Canvassers with no crew lead aren't in any team's report.** Give them a crew lead on the campaign's **Team** tab if their doors belong in one.
- A team can also have a report for **one walk list** only. Your client sees it as "Volunteer team · North".

## What the numbers mean

- **A team report's totals run from the campaign's first door through the last day you pick**: everything that team's crews have done, not only your dates. The first day only decides what shows as new ("+412 this week"). See [Does a report count only the dates I pick?](report-totals-and-dates)
- **Each team report counts that team's own work.** If both teams reached the same home or the same voter, it counts in both reports, and each team report says so: "A home both teams visited counts in each team's report."
- **So team reports don't add up to the whole campaign.** The whole-campaign report counts that home once.
- **Doors marked at the office don't count toward any team.**
- **Team reports never show a door goal.** Goals are for the whole campaign.
- **The team's map** shows the doors that team knocked, with that team's own results and answers.

If only one canvasser knocked for a team in the period, the builder warns you before you publish: the report then shows one person's work.

**Before you publish** on a team report also lists **Door visits this period** (a Latest progress team report shows just how many canvassers have knocked for the team since the campaign began): a row for each team (this report's team first, and "Together" when there are two or more), then the whole campaign's door visits (or the walk list's), how many of them more than one team made (they count in each team's report), and how many no team made (canvassers with no crew in a team, or doors entered in bulk). It's how you see why the teams' numbers and the whole campaign's don't line up. Your client never sees it.

## When the team changes

- **Edit team** (on the team's tab) renames it, changes its crews, or ends it. Ending a team keeps its published reports exactly as they were sent.
- **Continue this team** (on an ended team's tab) brings it back: pick the crews it covers from now on, and it takes new reports again.
- **Moving a canvasser onto another crew moves their past doors with them**, and so does adding someone back to the campaign (their earlier doors there move to the crew they rejoin, or to no crew), so a team's next report can count differently. On the web, the confirm that moves them names the client report whose numbers change. The phone says how many doors move, but not which report.
- **If a crew lead leaves the campaign**, the team's next draft stops and asks what to do. By default her crew's past doors **stay in the team** while those canvassers have no crew. You can also remove them (it shows how many doors that takes out), change the team's crews, or end the team.

**Reports you've already published don't change** when a team does. If you refresh one, it's rebuilt with the same crews it was built with, so a report your client already has never quietly starts covering someone else.
