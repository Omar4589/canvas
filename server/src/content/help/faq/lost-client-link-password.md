Press **Share** on that report, on the campaign's **Client Reports** tab. The link's password is under its address, as dots: **Show** puts it on screen, and **Copy password** copies it. Org admins and the campaign's team leads can do this any time, because Doorline keeps the password, encrypted, for exactly this.

## When it can't be shown

Share says "This link's password can't be shown. To have one you can see, change it — then send your client the new password." That's a link made before Doorline kept passwords (it only ever kept a scrambled check of them), or a moment when Doorline can't open its copy. Press **Change password**, choose **Make one up for me** or **Type my own**, and send your client the new password. Changing it signs out anyone using the old one.

If other links of the campaign use the same password, Change password offers to change those too, already ticked, for example "Also change it on the 3 other links that use this password (Whole campaign, Paid team)", so your client keeps one password for all of them.

## Before you type your own

Under **Type my own**, Share says: "Everyone who manages this campaign can see this password later. Don't use one you use anywhere else." That's every org admin and every team lead on the campaign, including a client who has a team-lead account.

## When someone who manages the campaign leaves

They still know every password they could see. Press **Change password** in **Share** on your newest report, keep **Also change it on the other links** ticked, and send your client the new password. If another tab uses a different password (say, for someone else), change that one the same way.

Doorline staff can't see link passwords, even during a support session.

For everything about links, see [Client reports and share links](client-reports).
