Yes. On the **Turf Cutting** page, open **Exclude doors** and tick **Visited in the last [7] days — since [date]**. Every door a canvasser visited since that date is left out of the cut. Type a number of days or pick a date from the calendar; the other one follows.

People use it to:

- **Start the next pass while the last one is still finishing** — cut the whole walk list, minus anything knocked this week.
- **Give not-homes a rest** — tick **Not home** under Target doors, and leave out the ones knocked in the last few days.
- **Space out visits** when a client asks you not to knock the same door again too soon.

It works with **Target doors** ticks or on its own. On its own, it means the whole walk list except the doors visited recently.

## What counts as a visit

Any result a canvasser recorded at the door — Not home, Surveyed, Refused, Lit dropped, Restricted, No soliciting, Wrong address, Not a target voter — in **any pass or walk list of this campaign**.

It does **not** count notes, desk marks (a home or book marked restricted from the office or an admin's phone — **Exclude restricted-access homes** handles those), entries removed with Unknock, or other campaigns' knocks (campaigns never share doors). A paper day counts only once someone types the results into the app, and then from the day they were typed.

## How the days work

Days follow the **campaign's** time zone. "Last 7 days" means today plus the six days before it — the same as the dashboards' **7 days** button — and "since Oct 25" starts at midnight on Oct 25. The page always shows the date, so there's no guessing.

## Two things to know

- **Doors are checked when you click Generate.** If the last pass is still being knocked, a door knocked after you cut stays in the new books. Cut (or re-generate the drafts) close to when you activate.
- **The pass remembers the date.** **Add as new book** later in the pass leaves out the same doors, and a line over the books says so. Coming back after a reload, the panels open empty, but a line above them offers the pass's last conditions with **Use them again**, and **Generate** asks before cutting without them.
