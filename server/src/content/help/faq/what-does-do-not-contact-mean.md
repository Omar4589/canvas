A voter with a **Do not contact** badge has asked never to be contacted by your organization —
usually they told a canvasser or called in, and an admin recorded it.

**What to do at the door: skip that person.** Their survey is disabled in the app, and even a
submission that slips through (say, one queued while you were offline) won't be saved. You can
still talk to the *other* people at the address — the door itself stays on your list as long as
anyone there is still contactable.

If **everyone** at an address is flagged, the whole door drops off your map and books
automatically — you'll never be routed there. No action needed from you.

The badge follows the person across every campaign, so you may see it on someone who asked to be
left alone during a previous election. **Only org admins** set and remove the flag, from the web
console — team leads can't, precisely because the flag reaches every campaign at once, not just
their own. If a resident asks you to never come back, record it in your door note and tell your
admin so they can flag the voter.
