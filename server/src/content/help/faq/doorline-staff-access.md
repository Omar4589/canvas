Not casually — and never silently.

Doorline personnel **cannot open your organization's voter or canvassing data** in the product without
an individual **support access grant**. A grant names a written reason, covers one organization at a
time, and **expires automatically within hours**. Access under a grant is designed to be recorded in
an **audit log** — the staff member, the organization, what kind of data was accessed, when, and the
stated reason — and those records are retained.

The usual reason is support you asked for: a bug report or a data question that can only be answered
by looking at your account.

This is about Doorline's own staff. Your organization's admins, team leads, and canvassers working in
your org are simply using the product — their permissions are governed by their role, not by grants.

The one exception is Doorline's own internal demo organizations, which contain only fictional data —
never a customer's — so staff work in those directly.

The full commitment is in our [Privacy Policy](https://doorline.app/privacy).
