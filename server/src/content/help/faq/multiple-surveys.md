Yes. A campaign has a default survey, but each [walk list](walk-lists) can **override** it with its own survey (or inherit the campaign's). At the door, a canvasser is always shown the survey that belongs to that door's walk list.

That means one campaign can run different questionnaires side by side — for example, a persuasion survey on one walk list and a GOTV script on another. Set the override from the campaign's Survey tab or on the Walk Lists page.

See [Building and assigning surveys](surveys).
