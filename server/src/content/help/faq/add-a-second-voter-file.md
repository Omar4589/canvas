You don't need a new walk list or a new pass — you can add the new doors to your **existing** walk list. It's two steps, and the second one is the one people miss.

1. **Claim the new doors.** New addresses always land in **Intake**. Open your walk list → **Claim** → **Claim all Intake**. If the second file's addresses are the only thing in Intake, this grabs exactly those.
2. **Cut them into a book.** On **Turf Cutting** for that walk list's active pass, you'll see "N doors not in any book" → **Add as new book** (a supplemental book — runs in the background with a progress bar) → **Accept** → **Assign**.

> Heads up: Claiming alone doesn't put doors in front of canvassers — a door is only visible in the field once it's cut into a book and assigned. That's why step 2 matters.

**New voters at addresses your walk list already owns:**

- If that door **hasn't been knocked yet**, the new voter rides along automatically — the canvasser sees them when they reach the door. No steps needed.
- If that door was **already knocked or surveyed**, it reads "done," so nobody is sent back — the new voter would be missed. To catch this, tick **"Revisit already-worked homes that gain a new voter"** on the import review screen. The app then collects those homes into a **saved search** ("New voters — <file>"), and the import summary shows **Create revisit walk list →**. Click it — the new-walk-list form opens with that saved search already picked under **Doors** — create the walk list, confirm the **move** when the app shows you what each existing list gives up (it snapshots their books first, so it's undoable), cut books, and go back. Because it's a fresh round, the revisit **counts as a new knock**, and the first knock's history is kept.
