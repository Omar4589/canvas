You don't need a new walk list or a new pass — you can add the new doors to your **existing** walk list. It's two steps, and the second one is the one people miss.

1. **Claim the new doors.** New addresses always land in **Intake**. Open your walk list → **Claim** → **Claim all Intake**. If the second file's addresses are the only thing in Intake, this grabs exactly those.
2. **Cut them into a book.** On **Turf Cutting** for that walk list's active pass, you'll see "N doors not in any book" → **Add new doors** (a supplemental book) → **Accept** → **Assign**.

> Heads up: Claiming alone doesn't put doors in front of canvassers — a door is only visible in the field once it's cut into a book and assigned. That's why step 2 matters.

New voters at addresses your walk list **already owns** ride along automatically — no steps needed.
