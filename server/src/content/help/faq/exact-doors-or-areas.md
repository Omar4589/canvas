Every client report's map can show **exact doors** (the default) or **areas**.

**Exact doors** puts a dot at each home your team reached, colored by how the door went. Your client can tap one to see its street address and the answers to the questions you put on the map. Choose it when your client should see exactly where the team has been.

**Areas** shades fixed hexagons instead, each with how many doors were reached there and how they went. **Areas never show an address.** Choose it when door-level answers feel too exact for this client. You pick the size:

- **Small areas**, about 1,100–1,400 ft across;
- **Large areas**, about 0.55–0.7 mi across.

## Survey answers in area view

Only pick-one questions can show answers by area. Even then, a question shows its answers area by area only when **every** area has at least five answers to it. If one area has fewer, that report shows only the question's overall chart, and the builder tells you so: under **Before you publish** on a draft, and on a published report in the **Switch to areas** confirm, the **Refresh numbers** review and the **Worth a look** box. **Large areas usually fix it.**

The hexagons are see-through so the streets show under them, and the legend's colours match them as they look on the map.

Two things still worth knowing:

- An area where every home that answered gave the same answer tells your client how each of them answered.
- Your client sees every report on the link. Another week's report, a team or walk-list report, or a report with exact doors can narrow things down. The builder lists any that could.

How each door went (surveyed, didn't answer, and so on) shows for every area, even one with a single home.

## Switching

- **On a draft**, choose **Exact doors** or **Areas** under **Map**, then press **Recompute** before you publish.
- **On a published report**, **Switch to areas** is part of **Edit**. Your client keeps seeing exact doors until the areas are ready, then sees "Edited". On a report that's taken down, they see the areas when you put it back up.
- **Going back to exact doors**, or changing the area size, on a published report means **Refresh numbers**. The dialog warns you that exact doors put street addresses back in front of your client.
