No. **A report's totals always run from the campaign's first door through the last day you pick.** The first day only decides what shows as new: the "+412 this week" under each headline number.

So there's no separate "everything up to a date" option to look for. Every report already is one: pick dates that end on the day you want, and the totals are everything through that day.

## What that looks like

- **The usual weekly report.** You send one every week. Each shows the campaign's totals so far, through the last day of that week, plus what was added that week.
- **One team's totals.** In **New report**, choose **A team** and pick it (say, your paid team). Its report counts everything that team's crews have done since the campaign's first door, through the last day you pick, plus what they added in your dates. **Whole campaign** means everyone's work, not every date.
- **Any dates you like.** Last week, This week, or **Pick dates**: whichever you choose, the totals run from the beginning. Two weeks only changes the new figure to "+812 in these 14 days".

New report says this above the date choices, naming what the report covers: "Your report shows totals for Paid team, from the campaign's first door through the last day you pick. The first day only decides what shows as new, like “+412 this week”."

## What your client reads

Under **Activity at a glance**, the report and its PDF say "Totals since the campaign began, through Oct 25." Everything else on the report counts the same way, through its last day: the breakdowns, voter groups, survey charts, door goal and map. Only the "+" figures belong to the report's own dates.

If you build a report before its last day is over, that line reads just "Totals since the campaign began.", and the report says "Numbers as of Oct 24 — the period was still running."

**A team's totals follow who's on its crews now.** Move a canvasser to another crew and their past doors go with them (see [I moved someone to another team — what happens to the doors they already knocked?](move-a-canvasser-to-another-team)). Reports you've already published don't change unless you refresh their numbers.
