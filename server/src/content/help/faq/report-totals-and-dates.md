No. **A report's totals always run from the campaign's first door through the last day you pick.** The first day only decides what shows as new: the "+412 this week" under each headline number.

So every report already counts everything up to its last day. When you want just the totals so far, with nothing marked as new, pick **Latest progress · all time, through today** in New report, or leave **Pick dates**' first day empty for everything up to a day you choose.

## What that looks like

- **The usual weekly report.** You send one every week. Each shows the campaign's totals so far, through the last day of that week, plus what was added that week.
- **Just the latest numbers.** Your client asks how many doors you've knocked, how many surveys, how much support. **Latest progress** gives everything through today, with no "+" figures, and your client's report reads "All time". Doing it every week? **Next report** on your newest Latest progress report makes the next one through today, with your settings and notes copied.
- **One team's totals.** In **New report**, choose **A team** and pick it (say, your paid team). Its report counts everything that team's crews have done since the campaign's first door, through the last day you pick. With Latest progress, that's everything they've done so far. **Whole campaign** means everyone's work, not every date.
- **Any dates you like.** Last week, This week, or **Pick dates**: whichever you choose, the totals run from the beginning. Two weeks only changes the new figure to "+812 in these 14 days".

New report says this above the date choices, naming what the report covers: "Your report shows totals for Paid team, from the campaign's first door through the last day you pick. The first day only decides what shows as new, like “+412 this week”." With Latest progress picked, it ends "…through today, with no “+412 this week” figures."

## What your client reads

Under **Activity at a glance**, the report and its PDF say "Totals since the campaign began, through Oct 25." Everything else on the report counts the same way, through its last day: the breakdowns, voter groups, survey charts, door goal and map. Only the "+" figures belong to the report's own dates. A Latest progress report says "Totals since the campaign began, as of Oct 28." ("through Oct 28" once its numbers are refreshed after that day) and shows no "+" figures at all.

If you build a dated report before its last day is over, that line reads just "Totals since the campaign began.", and the report says "Numbers as of Oct 24 — the period was still running."

**A team's totals follow who's on its crews now.** Move a canvasser to another crew, or add someone back to the campaign, and their past doors there go with them (see [I moved someone to another team — what happens to the doors they already knocked?](move-a-canvasser-to-another-team)). Reports you've already published don't change unless you refresh their numbers.
