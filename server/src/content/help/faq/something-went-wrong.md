When something you tap in the Doorline phone app fails, the app keeps running and shows a one-line banner just under the top bar: **"Something went wrong"**, with **Restart** and **✕**. If it happens again, it counts: *"Something went wrong (3)"*.

**Did I lose anything?** Usually not.

- Doors and surveys you already saved are safe — they're on the server, or waiting safely on your phone.
- The thing you just tapped may not have gone through, so check it. On a door, look at the door's result.
- If you tapped something inside a pop-up sheet, the banner shows once the sheet closes.

**What to do:**

- **Tap the words** to see the details: when it happened, which version of Doorline you have, and the error. Take a screenshot.
- **Canvassers:** send the screenshot to your team lead or admin.
- **Team leads and admins:** send it to Doorline support at hello@doorline.app, with the time and what was tapped.
- **Tap Restart** if anything looks stuck. Doorline shows *Restarting Doorline…* and reopens in a few seconds, still signed in — on a weak signal it can take a minute. It waits for any knock that's still saving, and it isn't offered while you're on a door or in a survey, or while the app is asking for your password — finish that first. If you can't, or if it keeps saying it's still saving, close Doorline and open it again.
- **Tap ✕** to hide the banner.

**On a door, nothing takes the door away.** If the door's buttons stop working, go back to the map and restart Doorline — or close and reopen it if Restart isn't offered — then record the door again.

**"This door hit a problem."** If a door screen — or a survey, or a practice survey — crashes, only that screen is replaced. Tap **Go back**, or **Try again**. Answers on that screen that weren't saved are gone; doors and surveys you already saved are not.

**"Something crashed"** is the full screen. It appears when another screen can't be shown, or when errors pour in non-stop away from the doors.

- **Restart Doorline** reopens the app in a few seconds, usually still signed in. If Doorline was about to sign you out anyway (for example, your password was changed somewhere else), the screen says so: you'll sign in again and your houses download again, so you need signal.
- **Reset & go home** is only for when Restart brings you straight back to this screen. You'll choose your organization and campaign again, and your houses download again, so you need signal. Saved work waiting to send is kept.

**"Doorline hit a problem"** with a Restart button means the app's outer frame failed. Tap **Restart Doorline**.

**A gray screen, or the app closed?** Close Doorline completely and reopen it. Your saved work is safe. If it keeps happening, tell your team lead or admin what you were doing when it happened.

**Team leads and admins:** when a canvasser sends you a screenshot of the details, forward it to Doorline support with the time and what they tapped.

For the banner that says a new version is ready, see [restarting for a new version](app-asks-to-restart).
