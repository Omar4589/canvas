**All of their doors move with them** — including the ones they knocked before you made the change.

A canvasser's work counts for whoever their coordinator is **right now**. So the moment you set or change someone's coordinator, their whole history follows: every campaign, all the way back.

## The case this is really for

You add a new canvasser on a busy morning and forget to put them on a crew. They knock 47 doors. You notice at lunch and set their coordinator.

**Those 47 doors move onto that team.** You don't end up with a permanent pile sitting in "No coordinator" because of a setup step you missed at 7am.

## The trade-off, stated plainly

Because history follows the current crew, **a team number you gave someone last month can change if you reassign a person afterwards.**

Three things soften that:

- **The app shows you the damage first.** Before it saves, it tells you how many doors and surveys will move, and between which two teams.
- **It's reversible.** Set the coordinator back to what it was and the numbers return exactly as they were.
- **It's recorded.** Every change keeps a note of who moved whom, from which team to which, and how many doors went with them.

## What does *not* change

- **The campaign's own totals** and your coverage. Only the split *between* teams moves.
- **Your connection and contact rates** for the campaign.
- **Your bill.** Billing counts doors, not teams — it has never cared which crew someone was on.

## Two things worth knowing

**Somebody leaving doesn't move anything.** Deactivating an account, removing someone from a campaign or the organization, or a canvasser deleting their own account all leave their doors exactly where they are — on the team they worked for. Only a coordinator change moves work between teams. See [A canvasser quit — do their knocks still count?](canvasser-left-the-team).

**Giving a coordinator to someone who runs a crew moves their own doors.** If a team lead reports to somebody, that lead's *personal* doors move onto their coordinator's team. Their crew's doors stay with them. The confirmation tells you when this applies.

## Doing it

Either place works, and they behave identically:

- **Users** → open the person → **Coordinator**.
- A campaign's **Team** tab → open the person → **Crew (coordinator)**.

Both show the confirmation before anything moves. Note that a crew is set for the **whole organization**, not per campaign — so a change on the Team tab of one campaign moves that person's doors in your other campaigns too.
