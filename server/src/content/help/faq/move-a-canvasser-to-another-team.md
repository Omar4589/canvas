**All of their doors move with them** — including the ones they knocked before you made the change.

A canvasser's work counts for whoever their coordinator is **right now**. So the moment you change someone's crew on a campaign, their history on **that campaign** follows: all the way back to their first door there.

**Only that campaign, though.** A crew belongs to a campaign, not to your organization — the same canvasser can walk one race for Asa and another for Frank, and each campaign keeps its own answer. Changing her crew on one moves nothing on the other.

## The case this is really for

You add a new canvasser on a busy morning and forget to put them on a crew. They knock 47 doors. You notice at lunch and set their coordinator on that campaign's Team tab.

**Those 47 doors move onto that team.** You don't end up with a permanent pile sitting in "No coordinator" because of a setup step you missed at 7am.

## The trade-off, stated plainly

Because history follows the current crew, **a team number you gave someone last month can change if you reassign a person afterwards.**

Four things soften that:

- **It's contained.** Only the campaign you changed is affected. Whatever you've quoted for your other campaigns stays put.
- **The app shows you the damage first.** Before it saves, it tells you how many of *that campaign's* doors and surveys will move, and from which teams. If a crew involved belongs to a team that has client reports, the confirmation on the web also names that team: *"This also changes the numbers in the Paid team client report."* (The phone's confirmation doesn't show that line.) Reports you've already published don't change until you refresh their numbers.
- **It's reversible.** Set the coordinator back to what it was and the numbers return exactly as they were.
- **It's recorded.** Every change keeps a note of who moved whom, on which campaign, from which team to which, and how many doors went with them.

## What does *not* change

- **Your other campaigns.** A crew change on one campaign never reaches the rest of them.
- **The campaign's own totals** and your coverage. Only the split *between* teams moves.
- **Your connection and contact rates** for the campaign.
- **Your bill.** Billing counts doors, not teams — it has never cared which crew someone was on.

## Three things worth knowing

**Somebody leaving doesn't move anything.** Deactivating an account, removing someone from a campaign or the organization, or an account being deleted (by its owner or by an admin) all leave their doors exactly where they are — on the team they worked for. Only two things move work between teams: a crew change, and someone coming back to the campaign (below). See [A canvasser quit — do their knocks still count?](canvasser-left-the-team). That goes for a coordinator too: if a coordinator's account is deleted, their team keeps the doors it has and shows their name for 180 days, then *Deleted user*. Their crew has no coordinator until you set one — and setting one is a crew change like any other, so those people's doors on that campaign move to the new team.

**You can still fix the crew of someone you switched off.** A deactivated canvasser is still on the campaign's team, so their crew is still yours to set, and their doors still move when you set it.

**Someone coming back to the campaign brings their doors with them.** A person you *removed* keeps their doors on the team they were on until they're added back. Adding them back works like a crew change: pick their crew while you add them, and their earlier doors on that campaign move to it — or to No team if you pick none. That's the same for everyone, admins and team leads included, and it's also what happens when a book puts an admin or a team lead back on the team. The add step says how many doors they have there and picks their old crew for you when that coordinator can still run one.

**Giving a coordinator to someone who runs a crew moves their own doors.** If a team lead reports to somebody, that lead's *personal* doors move onto their coordinator's team, on that campaign. Their crew's doors stay with them. The confirmation tells you when this applies. Running a crew is per campaign too: somebody who runs a crew on one campaign but knocks on another *without* one there shows up under "No team" on that second campaign, which is the right answer — they don't run a team there.

## Doing it

**There's one place: a campaign's Team tab.** Open the campaign → **Team** → click the person, and pick their **crew** in the panel. The confirmation appears before anything moves, and the door count it quotes is that campaign's. To move several people at once, tick them and use **Move to crew…** — the same check, for all of them.

**If a change didn't finish.** A crew change cut off partway — say the server restarted while it saved — shows on the person's panel as **Finish it**, and in the campaign's History as *Didn't finish*. Finish it completes it, with the count first. The same button appears when someone on a crew has doors that still count for another team. On the phone, the person's sheet gets **Finish it** with an app update.

**If two people change the same person at once**, the second is told instead of silently winning: *"Another change for Sam is still finishing — try again in a minute or two"*, or *"Their crew changed since you looked."*

The org **Users** page no longer sets crews. Open a member's profile there and you'll see a read-only list instead — one row per campaign they're on, the crew they're on there, and a link straight through to that campaign's Team tab. A single dropdown on someone's org profile couldn't be right for a canvasser working two campaigns under two different leads, which is exactly why it's gone.
