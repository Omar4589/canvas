The quickest way is **inline from a campaign's roster**: on the campaign's **Team** page, click **Add people** and enter their **email address**. If nobody is using it, you'll fill in their name, pick their **crew**, and their account is created — they get an **invitation email** with a set-password link (valid 72 hours), they choose their own password, and they're in. This adds the account and drops them onto that campaign, on their crew, in one step. Both **team leads** and **admins** can do this. (On the phone, **Add a canvasser** asks who the email belongs to first, the same way — it comes with an app update.)

The **temporary password field is optional**: leave it blank and the emailed invite is their way in. Type one only when the person can't get to their inbox — it can be simple and easy to read out loud, and they're required to replace it with their own strong password the first time they sign in. Emails never contain a password either way.

Admins can also add people org-wide from the **Users** page (**Add member**) — same idea, starting with the email, and you set the role (canvasser, team lead, or admin) there. For a canvasser you can check **several campaigns** at once, each with **its own crew**, or none to add them to your organization only — nothing is checked until you check it. On the phone, **More → Users → + New** with **All campaigns** picked works the same way (with an app update). An org admin can also add someone to more campaigns later from their profile (on the phone, with an app update).

> Heads up: a temporary password is valid for **72 hours**. Have the new person sign in and set their own password within that window — if it lapses, open them and click **Resend invite** to email them a fresh set-password link, or set a new temporary password if they can't get to their inbox.

**Never got the invite?** Open them and click **Resend invite**. They get a brand-new set-password link, good for another 72 hours. The button is for people who have **never signed in** (for an older account with no sign-in on record, it's under **More options**) — anyone who has already signed in has a working password and can use **Forgot password** themselves. Resending replaces any earlier link, so if they were part-way through setting a password from an old email, that one stops working.

**What if they already have a Door Line account?** Then that account is what gets added — you don't have to know in advance, and there's nothing to switch on. If the address belongs to **someone already in your organization**, you'll see their name and be asked to confirm before they're added to the campaign. If they worked this campaign before, the step says how many doors they have there and which teams those count for, and picks their old crew for you when that coordinator can still run one — their earlier doors move to the crew you pick, or to No team. If the address belongs to someone who has canvassed before **for a different organization**, the add simply goes through and the confirmation tells you who landed. Either way they keep the password they already use, so no new temporary password is issued, and if you typed a name or password the step says it wasn't used.

> **Read the confirmation.** When an address already has an account, that existing person is added and anything you typed — the name, a temporary password — is ignored, because it isn't ours to change. If the name in the confirmation isn't who you meant, you mistyped the address: remove them from the campaign and try again.

**Created a brand-new account with a mistyped email?** You can fix the address when you open them, and resend the invite. Or an **org admin** can delete the account (open the person; it's behind **More options**), and you add them again with the right address. Only ever delete an account that was just created by mistake. **Never delete someone who was linked** because their address already had an account: that's a real person's login. Take them off the campaign instead, and if they were added to your organization by mistake, an admin can use **Remove from org**.

**"Their account is switched off."** That's a colleague whose Door Line account has been deactivated. They can't be added until an **org admin** switches it back on, because that switch affects every campaign in the organization, not just yours. An org admin gets **Switch on** right in the step; if the person is already on this campaign, that's all it does — their crew stays as it was.

**Already on the campaign?** Then nothing changes — you'll see **Already here** with a link to open them. Their crew is changed from their panel.

Once the account exists, assign them a book so they can start knocking. See [Assigning canvassers](assigning-canvassers).
