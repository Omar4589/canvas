You can put it back. Archiving never deletes anything — no knocks, answers, notes, books or assignments.

## Right away: Undo

Right after you archive a walk list or a pass, a line at the top of the page — like *North archived — Pass 3 ended.* — has an **Undo** link. One click puts it all back: the walk list, its pass, and the same books for the same canvassers.

## Later: a walk list

Open the **Archived walk lists** section at the bottom of the Walk Lists page and click **Unarchive** on it. The box asks about the pass that archiving ended:

- **Unarchive and bring Pass 3 back** — the pass goes live again, and its books return to the same canvassers' phones the next time their map refreshes.
- **Unarchive only** — the walk list opens again and the pass stays ended. You can still bring it back later with its own **Bring back** button.

If the pass can't come back, the box says why and offers a plain **Unarchive**. For example, a survey campaign needs a survey attached before any pass can go live again.

## Later: a pass

Open the walk list's **Passes** panel — or pick the pass on Turf Cutting — and click **Bring back**. The box tells you what will happen before anything changes.

- **A pass that was live comes back live**, with the same books for the same canvassers. A pass that was a draft comes back as a draft.
- **A pass with no doors left in its books comes back as a draft** — say every door was moved into another walk list.
- **Bring back works until a newer pass on that walk list has gone live.** After that, the pass's row says why it can't come back. A pass that ended because the next pass went live never comes back — start a new pass to keep going.
- If its walk list is archived, unarchive the walk list instead (above).

## What canvassers see

The books come back on their phones the next time their map refreshes. Doors they knocked on that pass with no signal, and sent after you archived it, still count in that pass after the app update — nothing is missing from its numbers. Someone removed from the campaign in the meantime doesn't get their book back; it comes back unassigned.

## Who can do this

Org admins, and team leads on the campaigns they manage — the same people who can archive. Every archive, unarchive and bring back shows in the campaign's [History](who-changed-the-door-goal). More in [Walk lists](walk-lists) and [Passes](passes).
