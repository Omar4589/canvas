Yes — org admins can do it two ways.

**When you add someone.** On the **Users** page, click **Add member**. On the phone, go to **More → Users**, pick the **All campaigns** pill, and tap **+ New** (with an app update). Start with their email address:

- **Someone new** — fill in their name and pick a role. For a **canvasser**, you get a list of your active campaigns. **Nothing is checked until you check it** — it never puts anyone on every campaign. Each campaign you check gets **its own crew** (coordinator on the phone), starting at No crew. Leave them all unchecked and they join your organization only, ready to add later. A **team lead** gets the campaigns they run instead, and an **admin** gets no list, because admins already see every campaign.
- **Someone already in your organization** — you'll see their name and the same list, with the campaigns they're already on marked **on it**. If they worked a campaign before, it says how many doors they have there and picks their old crew when that coordinator can still run one. Their earlier doors there count for the crew you pick.

**One email per add, never one per campaign.** Someone new gets the invite to set their password, naming every campaign you put them on. Someone already in your organization gets one email listing the campaigns — unless they haven't set their password yet, in which case their invite is still their way in and the app shows their campaigns once they sign in.

**From their profile.** Open the person — their panel on the web (under **Everywhere in your organization**), or their profile on the phone (with an app update):

- **Add to campaigns** lists the active campaigns they aren't on yet. Check the ones you want, pick a crew for each, and add them all in one go.
- **Remove** beside a campaign takes them off it. It asks first: any books they hold there go back to the pool (a book they share with other canvassers stays with those canvassers), and every door they knocked still counts toward the campaign. If they run a crew there, it says how many people will be left with no coordinator.

A switched-off account has to be switched back on before you can add it to campaigns. Adding from a single campaign's **Team** page (or that campaign's pill on the phone) still works the same as before. See [How do I add a canvasser to a campaign?](assign-canvasser-to-campaign).
