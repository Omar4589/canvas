Open the campaign's **⋮ menu → History**. If it's the door goal you're wondering about, there's also a **History** link on the door-goal line at the top of the campaign's Home page.

**On your phone:** open the campaign and tap **Quick actions → History**. If the campaign has a door goal, there's a **History** row in the Door goal section too. It's the same feed, read-only.

You'll see a list, newest first:

> **Door goal** changed from **12,000 doors** to **9,000 doors**
> Lee Lead · Aug 12, 2026, 4:02 PM CDT
>
> **Goal date** changed from **not set** to **Oct 28**
> Ada Admin · Aug 10, 2026, 9:15 AM CDT

## What's recorded

- The **door goal** and **goal date**
- **Election Day**, the **early-voting window**, and the **key dates note**
- **Restricted doors on invoices** — the setting that changes what you bill
- **Archiving** and **reactivating** — archiving stops the billing clock
- The campaign's **name**, **type**, and **state**
- **Team reassignments**, including how many doors moved with the person

The **timezone** and the **attached survey** aren't recorded. A timezone change already makes itself obvious — every daily number on the campaign shifts, and you're warned before you confirm — and the survey in use is on the campaign's Survey tab.

## Why team moves are in there

Changing someone's coordinator moves **all of their past doors** onto the new team. That's a big number changing with nobody knocking a door, so it belongs in the same place as the settings changes: *"Cara: No coordinator → Bo Boss — 3,907 doors moved to Bo Boss."*

If a reassignment only half-completed, the entry is flagged — in red on the web, with an **incomplete** tag on the phone. That's the one case where your by-team numbers won't add up to the campaign total until it's re-run.

## Who can see it

Admins see it for any campaign. Leads see it for the campaigns they run — the same rule as everywhere else. Canvassers don't see it at all. It's read-only everywhere: there's nothing to edit on this screen, on either the web or the phone.

## This isn't the Audit page

Three things in this app are called "audit," and they answer different questions:

- **History** — who changed this campaign's **settings**. (This page.)
- **[Audit](audit)** — GPS quality flags on individual knocks: entries recorded far from the house, too fast, from a fake-GPS app.
- **[Timeline](page-timeline)** — who knocked what, when.

Looking for who *recorded a door* rather than who changed a setting? That's the Timeline.
