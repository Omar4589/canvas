Yes. Voter lists are always a little stale, and the person at the door is real either way. On a **survey campaign**, tap **＋ Add person** next to the voter list on the door screen (it's also offered on houses with no listed voters):

1. Enter their **first and last name** (required).
2. **Phone** and **email** are optional — only fill them in if the person would like the campaign to contact them back.
3. Tap **Add & take survey**. They're saved to that address immediately and their survey opens.

It works **offline** exactly like everything else — the add and the survey sync together when you're back in signal. The person stays on the door's list even if the survey doesn't happen.

**Don't see the button?** Either this is a lit-drop campaign (no voter lists at doors), or your campaign has limited adding people to **team leads and admins** — that's a campaign setting, not a bug. Record the door as usual and let your lead know who you met.

**For admins:** people added at the door are marked **"Added at the door"** (with who added them and when) in the web **Voters** directory — there's a filter to see just them. You can edit their details (including email) on their profile, and if an entry was a mistake, delete it from the profile: their survey answers and notes are removed, while the door visit itself stays recorded. If the same person later arrives in a voter-file import, the import creates a separate record under their real Voter ID — delete the door-added duplicate if you want just one.
