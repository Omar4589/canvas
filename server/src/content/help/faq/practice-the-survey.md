Yes. Open the menu (☰, top right) and tap **Practice the survey**. You'll see it on survey campaigns once you've picked your campaign. A lit-drop campaign has no survey, so it has no practice either.

The survey opens exactly as it looks at a door. It has the same questions and the same follow-ups that appear as you answer, the same read-aloud lines and closings, the same notes and links, and your own name where the script says it. On a script that shows one thing at a time, it has the same **Next**, **Skip** and **‹ Back**. Two things tell you it's practice:

- **The card at the top says Practice run**, with a blue **Practice** tag where a voter's name and the green **At Door** tag would be.
- **The last button says Finish practice** instead of **Save Response**. Tap it and the app tells you nothing was saved. **Practice again** starts over from the top; **Done** takes you back.

## Does practicing record anything?

No. Nothing you tap or type in practice is saved or sent. No house changes color, and nothing counts in your stats, your campaign's reports or anyone's bill. The app doesn't ask for your location, and it works with no signal, so you can rehearse anywhere: in the car, on the bus, the night before.

Practice the way you'd talk at the door. Try the answers you expect, and the awkward ones too, so you know where each one leads.

## Which survey does it show?

The one your books use, so what you rehearse is what you'll get at your doors. If your walk lists use different surveys, the app first shows a short list. Each one is named by walk list, so pick the one you're about to work. If you don't have any books yet, it shows your campaign's survey.

Your campaign may update the survey while you're out. Practice always shows the version your phone has now, the same one you'd get at the next door.

Don't use practice at a real door. Answers taken there aren't saved. When someone's at the door, tap their name on the house screen and use **Take survey**, so their answers count. See [Taking a survey at the door](canvasser-door-survey).
