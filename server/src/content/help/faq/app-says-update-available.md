Doorline sometimes needs you to install a newer version of the app itself — the kind of update
that comes from the **App Store** or **Google Play**. That's different from the small refreshes
the app handles on its own (those just show a quick
[restart banner](app-asks-to-restart), no store involved).

You'll see one of two things:

- **A banner at the top of the screen** saying a new version is available. This is just a
  heads-up — tap **Update** to go straight to the store, or **Later** to keep working and deal
  with it after your shift. The banner will come back the next time you open the app, until
  you've updated.
- **A full "Update Doorline" screen** that won't go away. That means the version on your phone
  is too old to keep using safely. Tap **Get the update**, install the new version from the
  store, and reopen the app — you'll pick up right where you left off.

Updating takes about a minute on a normal connection and never touches your work: everything
you've knocked and recorded is already saved.

If the update won't install — not enough space on your phone, or the store just won't finish —
tell your campaign admin. They can confirm the new version has finished rolling out to everyone.
