Yes. By default a restricted home isn't counted as a door on your invoice export, because nobody was actually reached there. But your canvasser still walked to it, and if you invoice your client **per door**, that trip is real work you may want to bill for.

## Turning it on

There are two places, and the campaign setting wins:

- **For your whole organization** — on the **Billing** page, tick *Count restricted homes as billable doors*. Every campaign follows this unless it says otherwise. (You need billing access to see this page.)
- **For one campaign** — open the campaign's edit screen and set **Restricted doors on invoices** to *Count them* or *Don't count them*. Leave it on *Use organization default* to follow the setting above.

It's **off** everywhere until you turn it on, and only org admins can change it — a team lead can't.

## What changes

A **Billable doors** number appears next to Doors on your dashboard, your Timeline, and your invoice export, and the export gains **Restricted doors** and **Billable doors** columns:

> Billable doors = doors knocked **+** restricted homes

## What doesn't change

This is the important part. Your **contact rate**, **survey rate**, **houses knocked**, and the **coverage bar** all stay exactly the same whether this is on or off. A locked gate answered nobody, so counting it as a contact would make those numbers say something untrue. Turning this on and off is safe and reversible — it changes how doors are *presented*, never what your canvassers recorded.

Two homes are deliberately left out:

- **Desk-marked homes.** If an admin marked a whole book — or a single home — restricted from the desk (Turf Cutting, the Map page, or the mobile admin app), nobody walked it — that's office work, so it isn't billed and isn't anyone's work.
- **Homes someone else reached.** If one canvasser marked a home restricted and another later knocked it, that's **one** door, counted once, as a knock.

## Does this change my Doorline bill?

No. Doorline charges a flat rate per active campaign per month and never counts your doors at all. This setting only affects the numbers you report to *your* client. See [How billing works](billing).

> One related change: a restricted home **does** start a campaign's billing month, the same as a knock would, because it means someone was out in the field. Marking a book — or a single home — restricted from your desk doesn't.
