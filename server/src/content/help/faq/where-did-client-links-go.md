Each report now has its own link, so links live with their reports: press **Share** on a report to make its link, see and copy its password, change it, extend the link, give it a new address or delete it.

Your old links to all your reports are listed above your reports under **Old links to all your reports**. While they still work, you can change their password, give them a new address or delete them there. Doorline is turning them off for everyone; once it does, the card says so, they no longer work, and Delete is all that's left. Each report now has its own link — press **Share** on it.

## Moving your client over

On each tab your client follows, press **Share** on the newest published report and send its link. To keep the password your client already has, choose **Type my own** and type it (it needs 12 or more characters; a shorter one can't be kept, so make up a new one and send it); on that client's other tabs, choose **Same as** that link.

## What else changed

- A new link has no label to type: Share and History name each link by its report.
- Each report's row says how its link is doing ("Link: active · expires Jan 25") and how often the report was opened.
- A password isn't shown only once any more: org admins and the campaign's team leads can press **Show** in Share any time. See [I lost a client link's password](lost-client-link-password).

For everything about links, see [Client reports and share links](client-reports).
