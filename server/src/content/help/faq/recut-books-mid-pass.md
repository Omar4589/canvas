Yes, but there are two very different situations.

**Just adding new/uncut doors?** Use **Add new doors** on the Turf Cutting page — it cuts only the doors that aren't in any book yet into new books, without touching your live books, assignments, or knocks. Then Accept and Assign them. This is almost always what you want.

**A true recut of the existing books?** On a pass with accepted books you can't just regenerate — you must **Discard** first. Discard snapshots everything for undo, then deletes the books and **all their assignments** and un-books the doors — but it **keeps** your knock history, door statuses, and surveys. Because the pass is live, it makes you type `discard` to confirm. Then you regenerate, Accept, and **reassign everyone**.

> Heads up: Knocked doors stay knocked after a recut — knocks are tied to the door and pass, never to a book — so coverage and billing are unaffected.
