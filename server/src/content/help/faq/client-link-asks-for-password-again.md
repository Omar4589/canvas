A client link remembers your client only for a while, and some changes sign everyone out on purpose. The usual reasons, most common first:

- **They didn't tick Keep me signed in on this device.** Without it, the link remembers them only until the browser tab closes. With it, they stay signed in for up to 30 days, though some phones clear it sooner.
- **It's a new report's link.** Each report has its own link, and each link asks for its password once, even when it's the same password as last week's.
- **Someone changed the link's password.** Every device is signed out at once, so send your client the new password. A password changed on several links at once signs everyone out of each of them.
- **They're on another browser or device**, or in a private window. Each one asks once.
- **They pressed Sign out on this device.**

**Check the tab's History first.** On the campaign's **Client Reports** tab, press **History**: a password change, a new address, a renewal or a link turned off shows there with who did it and when.

## If the page says something else

- **"This link isn't available"**: the link was given a new address, renewed, turned off or deleted, so the address your client has no longer works. A new address and a renewal keep the password: copy the new address from **Share** on that report and send it again. Deleting a report deletes its link too.
- **"This report was taken down or removed. Ask the person who sent it."**: that report is taken down. Put it back up and the link works again. (If the report was deleted, its link went with it, so the page says "This link isn't available" instead.)
- **"This report link has expired. Ask your campaign contact for a new one."**: press **Renew** in **Share** on that report. That gives the link a new address, which you send to your client.
- **"This link is locked after too many wrong passwords"**: someone typed 50 wrong passwords within a day. Anyone already signed in keeps reading, but nobody new can sign in until the lock ends or you press **Unlock now**. Fifty wrong passwords is never your client, so give the link a **New address** and send it again.
- **"Too many tries from this connection — wait 15 minutes"**: they guessed wrong several times in a row. Send the password again and have them wait a few minutes.
- **"This link no longer works. Ask the person who sent it for the link to the latest report."**: it's an old link to all your reports, which Doorline has turned off. Press **Share** on your newest report and send its link.

## Keeping it simple for your client

Send the link and the password separately, and tell them about **Keep me signed in on this device** so a phone they read on every week doesn't ask each time. Leave it off on a shared computer. Each new report's link can keep the same password (Share offers **Same as your last link**), so they only need to learn it once a season, unless you change it.
