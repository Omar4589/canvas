**Intake** is where doors sit when they're owned by **no walk list yet**. Every door belongs to exactly one walk list, or it's in Intake.

When you import a voter file, brand-new addresses always land in Intake — the import never assigns them to a walk list on its own. They're not canvassed until you **claim** them into a walk list (open a walk list → Claim → a saved search, or "Claim all Intake").

This is deliberate: it's the control point that lets you decide which operation owns which new doors.

See [Walk lists](walk-lists) and [Uploading a voter file](voter-imports).
