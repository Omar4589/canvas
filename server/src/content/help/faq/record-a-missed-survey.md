Yes. A canvasser who hit **Not home** when they actually did the survey can't always fix it themselves — re-recording away from the door flags their GPS. An org admin can do it from the **[Door Outcomes page](page-door-outcomes)**:

1. Filter to the entries (by canvasser, round, date range, or search the address) and tick them.
2. Change them to **Surveyed** and enter the answers — one answer set for the whole batch, or **Door by door** for per-household answers.

**Who gets the credit:** the canvasser who knocked. The entry keeps their name, time, GPS and round, so their numbers reflect their work. The answers carry a visible **"Entered by ‹you› on ‹date›"** stamp on the voter's record and in exports — a desk entry is never mistaken for a doorstep conversation — and they count in your rates like any other answer.

Recording answers this way never overwrites a real field answer — anyone who already answered that round is skipped — and every change is priced first and undoable. Full walkthrough: **[The Door Outcomes page](page-door-outcomes)**.

**The answer was recorded, but it's wrong?** That's a different tool on the same page, **Edit answers…**: it changes or takes out the wrong answer, keeps the survey and the earlier answers, and can be undone. See [A canvasser picked an answer they should have skipped](fix-one-survey-answer).
