They look similar in the field but count very differently:

- **Refused** — a person came to the door and turned you away. This **is** a billable knock and counts as a contact (you reached someone).
- **Restricted access** — you physically **couldn't reach** the door: a gated community, a locked building, no access. It's fully visible and counted as its own category, but it is **not** a billable knock (you never actually knocked).

Both stay on the map and in your counts. For restricted homes, an admin can choose to **exclude** them from the next round's books so nobody is sent back to a door no one can reach — while leaving the option to try again later if access opens up.
