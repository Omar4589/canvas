If a red notice at the top of your screen says **"Recording is paused for review. Call your team lead."**, your organization's automatic pause has stopped your recording for now.

It happens when the doors you save come in faster than anyone could get from one house to the next and knock — for example, doors saved from a moving car, or several doors saved at once from one spot. If your organization uses automatic pause, Doorline checks the pace of the doors you save, using only what each door already carries: when you saved it and where your phone was. Nothing follows you between doors.

## What to do

1. **Call your team lead.** They can see the doors behind the pause and go through them with you.
2. **Wait for someone on your team to unpause you.** Only a person can lift a pause — the app can't, and recording the doors again won't.
3. **Tap Check again** on the red notice once they tell you you're unpaused, then carry on. The app also notices on its own after a short while.

**Check again** needs signal. With none, it says *"Can't check right now — move to where you have signal and tap Check again."*

## While you're paused

- The door buttons are greyed out and **Add person** is hidden. Your map, your books, your stats and this Help Center all still work.
- If you were in the middle of a survey, your answers stay on the screen and **Save** tells you recording is paused. Keep that screen open: once you're unpaused, tap **Save** again. An **Add person** form you already had open works the same way.
- A survey you open while you're paused shows **Recording paused** instead of the questions, until you're unpaused.

## Your work is safe

The pause stops new doors only. Nothing you already saved is deleted or hidden — including doors still waiting to upload from a dead zone, which go up as usual. The door that set off the pause was saved too; when it happens the app says *"That door was saved. Recording is now paused for review. Call your team lead."* Whether any door needs fixing is for your team to decide with you.

## How to keep it from happening

- **Record each door at the door,** right after you knock — or right after you finish talking with someone — before you move on to the next house.
- **Don't save doors in a batch** later, from the car or the end of the street, even if you really knocked them. A batch of doors saved together looks exactly like doors nobody knocked.
- **Sign in only on your own phone.** Two phones signed in to one account save doors in two places at once, which reads as impossibly fast.

On an older version of the app, you may see the same words in an alert titled **Action not saved** (or **Survey not saved**) when you try to save a door. When the app says [a new version is ready](app-asks-to-restart), tap **Restart** to get the current one.
