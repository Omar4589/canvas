**Yes — every door they knocked still counts.** Their knocks stay in the campaign's totals, in your coverage, and on your bill, exactly as before. That's true whether you deactivated their account, removed them from the campaign, removed them from the org, or they deleted their account outright.

The reason is simple: a knock is a record of something that *happened*. Whether the person who made it can still log in is a completely separate question, and the two are deliberately kept apart. Nothing you can do to an account rewrites the work already done.

## So why can't I see them on the Timeline?

Because the Timeline opens on **today**, and they didn't knock today.

Switch the date range to **All time** and you'll see them — that view is campaign to date, so it shows every canvasser who has ever worked the campaign, departed or not, with their full totals.

## What removing them *does* change

Only what they were **holding**:

- **Remove from a campaign** hands back the books they had on that campaign, so you can give them to someone else. Books they hold on other campaigns are untouched. If other canvassers share those books, they keep them.
- **Deactivate the account** stops them signing in, org-wide. It's reversible, and it deliberately leaves their books alone — you may well switch them back on tomorrow.
