A canvasser only sees doors that are **cut into a book, assigned to them, on an active pass**. Run down this checklist:

1. **Is the pass active?** Draft and archived passes never show in the field.
2. **Are the books accepted?** Draft books aren't visible — Accept them first.
3. **Is a book assigned to that person?** No assignment, no doors.
4. **Have they refreshed?** New assignments show up on a full refresh — have them pull-to-refresh or reopen the campaign.

A common gotcha: newly **claimed** doors (e.g. from a second import) are owned but **not yet in a book**, so they're invisible until you cut them. See [I uploaded a second voter file](add-a-second-voter-file).
