**Most of the time, you don't need to.** **Remove from org** ends a person's access and keeps
everything your records need, including their name on reports and on a team. Delete an account when
the person asked to be deleted, or when the account shouldn't exist at all: a test account, a
duplicate, one made with the wrong email, or someone you hired who never started.

## Where to find it

Only org admins can delete an account. Team leads never see the option. It's tucked away on purpose:

- **On the web:** **Users** → click the person → in their panel, **More options ▸** → **Delete account…**
- **In the app:** **More → Users** → tap the person → **Account** → **More options** → **Delete
  account…** If you picked a campaign on the Users list, tap **Manage profile ›** first.

**Remove from org** sits right next to it: under **More options ▸** on the web, and in the same **Account** section in the app.

Then it asks you twice:

1. **A review.** It names the person and spells out what deleting them means, worked out from their
   own records: how many door results and surveys stay, which books come back, whether they lead a
   crew, and what to do about doors still on their phone. Choose **Continue** or **Cancel**.
2. **Type their email.** **Delete permanently** stays grey until what you type matches their email.
   **Back** returns you to the review and clears what you typed.

If the account can't be deleted, the review tells you why and what to do instead, and offers only
**Close**.

## Which accounts you can delete

Any account that **belongs only to your organization**:

- **Not your own.** To delete yours, use **Profile ▸ Delete account** in the app.
- **Not a Doorline staff account.**
- **Not in any other organization**, whether they're active there or switched off. The review won't
  say which organization. Use **Remove from org** to take them off yours. An organization they *used
  to* work for doesn't stop you.
- **Billing access protects the person who has it.** Only an admin who also has billing access can
  delete them or remove them from the organization. If you don't have billing access, those options
  don't show for them.
- **Not your only admin**, and not your only admin with billing access. Make someone else an admin,
  or give another admin billing access, first.

The app-store review logins in Doorline's demo organization can't delete anyone.

## What deleting does

- **Their login and contact details are erased right away:** email, phone, password, and the name on
  the account. They can never sign in again, and nobody can restore the account. Any invite or
  password link we emailed them stops working.
- **Nothing on your reports or your bill changes.** Every door result and survey they recorded stays.
- **They come off every campaign and walk list**, and lose any campaigns they ran as a team lead.
  Books only they were on go back to unassigned. Books they shared stay with the other canvassers on
  them.
- **Their email address is freed.** If they come back, add them again. They get a brand-new account,
  and their past work stays with the deleted one.

## Their name on your reports

**Doorline keeps their name privately for 180 days**, so it still shows on these screens:

- canvasser reports, on the web and in the app;
- team labels and by-team totals;
- the GPS audit, including its flags on the map;
- Survey Results downloads and every other export.

After 180 days those screens show **Deleted user** too. This holds even if you deactivated them
first. It also works the other way round: if someone who once canvassed for you is deleted somewhere
else later, your records keep their name for the same 180 days.

**Other screens show "Deleted user" right away:** the rest of the map, a door's history, survey
details and other detail screens.

## If they lead a crew

The people on their crew have no coordinator until you set one on the campaign's **Team** tab (tick
several and use **Move to crew…** to set it for all of them at once). The team keeps its doors and its
numbers, and shows their name for the same 180 days. Once you give someone a new crew, their doors on
that campaign move with them, like any crew change. If you'll keep reporting that team's numbers to a
client, use **Remove from org** instead: their account stays, so the team keeps their name.

## Doors still on their phone

A phone can hold doors it hasn't sent yet. **Before you delete, ask them to open the app somewhere
with signal**, so it sends everything it's holding. The review reminds you for any account that's been
used, and more firmly if their phone sent doors in the last week.

Until a phone update after Election Day, anything still on their phone after the deletion goes up
under **whoever signs in on that phone next**.

If they're **switched off (deactivated)**, their phone can't send anything. Whatever it tried to send
while they were switched off was refused and thrown away. If the doors it still holds matter, switch
them back on first (once billing is sorted, if your account is paused), let the phone send, then
delete.

## Your Users list afterwards

- **If the account was used** (they signed in, have anything on your records, or ever coordinated a
  crew), their row stays as **Deleted account**, with who deleted it and when: *Deleted Oct 23, 2026
  by Alex Kim*. The row keeps one action, **Remove from org**, which tidies it away without changing
  anything on your reports.
- **If nobody ever used it**, they disappear from your Users list completely.

Accounts made before mid-May 2026 always keep a **Deleted account** row, because Doorline only started
recording sign-ins then. You may also see *Deleted their own account* or *Deleted through Doorline
support* on rows deleted those ways.

## If your account is paused

While your organization is read-only (billing paused, ended, or a trial that ran out), you can still
delete an account, and switch someone's access off with **Deactivate** or **Remove from org**.
Switching access back on waits until billing is sorted.
