You keep **one result per door per round**. Recording a different result at a door **replaces** the one you recorded before — and if you took a survey there, it **deletes those survey answers**. That used to happen without a word, so now the app asks first. It works the same everywhere you record a result: the buttons on the door screen, the one-tap **Not home** (or **Lit dropped**) on each row of your door list and on each unit of an apartment building, and the survey screen.

## When it asks

It asks when the door already shows a result this round — yours or a teammate's, whatever its status says — and you pick a different one. You'll see one of three questions:

- **"Change this door's result?"** — for example, *"This door is already marked Not home this round. Record Refused instead?"* **Record** goes ahead; **Cancel** leaves the door as it was.
- **"This door is marked Not home"** — when you open a survey (**Take survey**, **Re-survey**, or **＋ Add person** → **Add & take survey**) at a door that already shows another result: *"This door is already marked Not home this round. Take a survey instead?"* **Continue** goes on to the survey; **Cancel** takes you back.
- **"You surveyed someone here"** — the stronger warning, when the change would delete answers you took at this door this round, including a survey still waiting to upload: *"You took 1 survey at this door this round. Recording "Refused" replaces your result for this door and deletes those answers. If you surveyed someone, the door is already done."* **Keep my survey** backs out with your answers safe; **Replace anyway** records the new result and deletes them.

The results named in each question are whatever the door shows and whatever you tapped.

**Already surveyed someone at the door?** Then it's done for this round. If someone else there declines afterwards, there's nothing more to record — tapping **Refused** (or any other door button) would delete the survey you took. Tap **Keep my survey**.

## When it doesn't ask

- **A fresh door** — nothing recorded there yet this round.
- **The same result again** — Not home twice, or **Re-record drop**.
- **Another voter at a door already marked Surveyed** — surveying the next person at the house doesn't ask. (Re-surveying a voter a teammate already surveyed this round is a different question — see [Taking a survey at the door](canvasser-door-survey).)
- **A door showing "Marked restricted by the office"** — that card already tells you to work the door normally; what you record replaces the mark.

The one exception: the **"You surveyed someone here"** warning shows before anything that would delete a survey you took at that door, even on a door that looks fresh. Your phone can briefly lose track of a door's status while a survey is still waiting to upload, and that's exactly when answers would be lost.

Once you tap **Replace anyway**, those answers are deleted, so the app won't warn you about them again. If the app then tells you the new result wasn't saved — say, your campaign had just turned that outcome off — nothing was deleted: your survey is still there, and you'll still get the warning next time.

So the everyday path — a fresh door, one tap — stays one tap. Only a change costs a second tap. For what each result means, see [Marking a door](canvasser-dispositions).

**Your app never asks, even when you change a result?** Then it's on an older version. When the app says a new version is ready, tap **Restart** ([what that banner means](app-asks-to-restart)); if it says an update is available, install it from the App Store or Google Play ([updating the app](app-says-update-available)).
