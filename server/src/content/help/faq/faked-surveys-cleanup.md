From the **[Door Outcomes page](page-door-outcomes)**: filter to that canvasser (a date range narrows it further, and on a survey campaign you can filter to a **specific answer** — "everyone they marked *Supporter*"), select the entries, and change them to **Not home**. The doors go back into play for the next round.

Before anything happens, the review step lists **exactly whose answers are about to be removed, by name** — and if you filtered by answer, it marks who matched your filter and who was simply surveyed at the same visit, because removing a visit's entry takes every answer recorded there, not just the matching one.

**Why removing answers can touch more people than matched your filter:** the entry being corrected is the whole visit — one canvasser, one door, one round. If they recorded two people at a door and only one gave the answer you filtered by, both answers go, and both are named in the review.

The answers are **archived, never destroyed** — they stay on each voter's record and can be restored, because in an investigation the removed answers are the evidence. Only that canvasser's answers are touched; a second canvasser's honest work at the same door survives. Every change is undoable, and the run remembers the filter that produced it.
