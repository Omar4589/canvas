Billing is simple and predictable, and it never gets in the way of work you've already done.

## What you pay

You're billed **per campaign, per month**. Your rate is agreed with your account manager, and it can differ from one race to the next — a statewide campaign and a small local one don't have to cost the same. Rates aren't shown in the app; ask your account manager any time.

A campaign starts billing with its **first field visit** — a knock, or a restricted home a canvasser walked to and couldn't get into. Setup, where you've created the campaign, imported voters, and cut turf but nobody's been out yet, is free. (Marking a book — or a single home — restricted from your desk isn't a field visit and doesn't start anything.) Billing then runs until you [archive the campaign](archive-vs-delete-campaign), so archiving is what stops it.

Each campaign on your Billing page shows **Billing started** with the date it began — or *billing not started*, if it hasn't yet.

The price never depends on how many doors you knock. If **you** invoice your own client per door, see [Can I bill for restricted doors?](bill-restricted-doors) — that setting changes your reports, not your Doorline bill.

## What "per month" means exactly

It depends on when the campaign first went out. Campaigns first out **on or after November 5, 2026** renew every month from the day of their first field visit. Campaigns first out before then bill by **calendar month**. Either way, nothing is ever billed by the day, and a campaign is never charged twice in the same month.

### Campaigns first out on or after November 5, 2026

**Billing renews on the date of your first knock.** Knock your first door on December 15th and the campaign renews on the 15th of every month — each renewal is one month. A campaign that starts on the 29th, 30th or 31st renews on the last day of shorter months (January 31st, February 28th, March 31st).

**Archive before the next renewal date and that renewal never happens.** First knock December 15th, archived February 3rd: you pay for the December 15th and January 15th renewals — two months.

**Archive on a renewal day or in the two days after it, and that renewal is free** — as long as nobody's been out since that renewal. Renewed on the 15th and archived on the 17th with nobody out since? That renewal is on us. Archive on the 18th, or with your team out on the 16th, and it bills.

**The first month always bills.** Because billing starts the day you first go out, you never pay for part of a month you didn't use — so there's no last-week rule for these campaigns.

Each renewal counts toward the calendar month it falls in, so a running campaign is charged once a month.

### Campaigns first out before November 5, 2026

These keep the **calendar month** — not 30 days counted from your first knock, and not an anniversary date. February costs the same as March. Two rules keep the edges fair:

**Start in the last week of a month and that month is free.** If your first knock lands in the **last 7 days** of a month, that month costs nothing — billing starts on the 1st of the next one. Knock your first door on January 28th and January is on us.

**Archive promptly and the new month is free too.** If you [archive a campaign](archive-vs-delete-campaign) in the **first 3 days** of a month *and nobody has been out that month*, you don't owe that month. If your team did knock on the 1st or 2nd, that's real work and the month bills normally.

What those two never do is add up to a free campaign: a campaign that knocked at least one door always pays for at least one month.

### For every campaign

- Archive on **March 20th**? On calendar months, March bills in full. On monthly renewals, a campaign that renews on the 15th pays for March 15th, and April's renewal never comes.
- Months and renewal dates follow **your campaign's timezone**, so a 9pm knock on the last day of the month counts for that day and that month.
- Between the start and the archive, **a campaign bills every month whether or not anyone knocked.** A quiet month still bills — there's no way to pause a campaign and skip one.

That last point is why we show a reminder to archive a campaign once its election day has passed. Archiving is what stops the billing. Nothing is deleted when you archive, and you can reopen it later if you need to.

Your **Billing** page shows which campaigns are canvassing this month (and, for campaigns that renew monthly, the day each renews on), which are still in free setup, and which are free on the last-week rule — so you can always see what's counting and what isn't. (See [Who can see the Billing page?](who-sees-billing) — it's limited to specific admins.)

## Looking back at previous months

Your Billing page keeps a **Month by month** history — the last 12 months by default, up to 24. For each month it shows how many campaigns were billing, how many doors were covered, and how many were knocked. Open a month to see the campaigns behind those numbers: when each started billing, its doors and knocks, and — for any that were free that month — why (still in setup, started in the last week, archived with nobody out, or, for a campaign that renews monthly, archived before its renewal or within 3 days of it).

This is the page to check when you're putting together your own invoice for a past month, or double-checking one you've already been sent.

Rates and amounts still aren't shown anywhere in the app; what a month cost is a conversation with your account manager.

### Doors vs knocks

**Knocks** are doors a canvasser actually knocked. **Doors** is the number you'd invoice from — the same figure, unless you've turned on **Count restricted homes as billable doors** at the bottom of the page, in which case it also includes homes a canvasser walked to but couldn't get into (a locked gate, a secured building). Flip that setting and the Doors column moves with it. It never changes what Doorline charges you.

## Your free trial

Every new organization gets a **free trial** with full access — **7 days by default, though your account manager can set a longer one** (say two weeks) when they set you up. You'll see a countdown in the last few days, and the trial can be extended anytime if you need more time.

## How payment works

Payment is handled **outside the app** — one of our account managers invoices you directly, so there's no card to enter or checkout to complete. Your job is just running campaigns; the billing side is looked after for you.

## If a payment is late

Nothing you've done is ever lost, and access winds down gently:

- **Past due.** You keep full access. You'll just see a reminder banner while an invoice is outstanding — a nudge, not a lockout.
- **Read-only.** If an account stays unresolved, it can switch to read-only: you and your team can still view everything you've built, but recording knocks, importing voters, and cutting turf pause until it's sorted. Canvassers already in the field get a short grace window, so a day's work is never interrupted.

While you're read-only you can still **export** your data, and you can still **switch someone's access off** — **Deactivate**, **Remove from org**, or [delete their account](delete-an-account) — so a person who leaves doesn't keep their access while billing is sorted out. Switching someone back on waits until it is, and so do adding people, crew changes and taking someone off a campaign.

As soon as billing is squared away, everything switches back on — nothing is deleted.

> Tip: Demo and internal organizations are always free — trials and suspension don't apply to them.
