Getting canvassers into the field is two steps: put them on the campaign's team, then hand them books.

## First, add people to the campaign

Being in your organization isn't the same as being on a campaign. Each campaign has its own **team** — the people who can work it. That team does two things: it decides who sees the campaign in the mobile app, and it's the only pool you can assign books to. You can't give a book to someone who isn't on the team.

Manage it on the campaign's **Team** page. Click **Add people** and start with their **email address** — that's the whole flow, whether they already work with you or you're hiring them right now. If the address belongs to someone already in your organization, you'll be asked to confirm and they join the campaign; if it doesn't, you'll fill in their name and their account is created on the spot, with an email inviting them to set their own password. Either way you pick their **crew** before they're added. It works the same on the web console and in the mobile admin app (on the phone, with an app update). Click any team member to open their panel — their activity in this campaign, their crew, their details and password (for canvassers), and **Remove from this campaign** under **More options**. See [add a canvasser account](add-a-canvasser-account).

The page lists **this campaign's** people. Org admins also get **From your organization** in the same Add people window: search, tick everyone you want (up to 500), pick one crew for all of them, and add — for staffing a new campaign in bulk. Someone who worked this campaign before brings their earlier doors here to the crew they rejoin, or to No team if you pick none.

Org admins can also start from the person instead of the campaign. On the **Users** page, **Add member** (or **+ New** on the phone's **All campaigns** pill, with an app update) asks for the email, then lets you check the campaigns to put them on, each with its own crew. And a person's panel (web, under **Everywhere in your organization**) or profile (on the phone, with an app update) has **Add to campaigns**, for the campaigns they aren't on yet, and **Remove** beside each campaign, which asks first because their books there go back to the pool.

## Then assign their books

Once people are on the team, assign books on **Turf Cutting**. Select the books, pick who walks them, and choose how to spread the work: give **everyone** the same books, split the **books** evenly between people, or split by **doors** so each person gets a similar number of doors to knock. See [turf and books](turf-and-books).

## The natural order

Because assignment is team-only, the easy flow is: add your crew first, cut turf, then assign. A canvasser has to be on the team before you can give them a book or pre-add them to a [walk list](walk-lists) — neither adds them to the team. The one exception is org admins and team leads: taking a book (or an admin handing them one) puts them on the team, so a lead who also walks doesn't have to be added first. That counts as coming back to the campaign, so their earlier doors there move to No team; set their crew from their panel afterwards.

> Tip: You don't have to wait for turf to be cut. Pre-add your whole crew to the campaign now, and the books are ready to hand out the moment they're drawn.
