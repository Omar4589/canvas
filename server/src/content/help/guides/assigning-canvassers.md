Getting canvassers into the field is two steps: put them on the campaign's team, then hand them books.

## First, add people to the campaign

Being in your organization isn't the same as being on a campaign. Each campaign has its own **team** — the people who can work it. That team does two things: it decides who sees the campaign in the mobile app, and it's the only pool you can assign books to. You can't give a book to someone who isn't on the team.

Manage it on the campaign's **Team** page. Click **+ Add someone** and start with their **email address** — that's the whole flow, whether they already work with you or you're hiring them right now. If the address belongs to someone already in your organization, you'll be asked to confirm and they join the campaign; if it doesn't, you'll fill in their name and their account is created on the spot, with an email inviting them to set their own password. It works the same on the web console and in the mobile admin app. Click any team member to open their profile — their activity in this campaign, their crew, their details and password (for canvassers), and a button to remove them. See [add a canvasser account](add-a-canvasser-account).

The page lists **this campaign's** people. Org admins also get a searchable list of everyone in the organization underneath, for staffing a new campaign in bulk.

## Then assign their books

Once people are on the team, assign books on **Turf Cutting**. Select the books, pick who walks them, and choose how to spread the work: give **everyone** the same books, split the **books** evenly between people, or split by **doors** so each person gets a similar number of doors to knock. See [turf and books](turf-and-books).

## The natural order

Because assignment is team-only, the easy flow is: add your crew first, cut turf, then assign. Two shortcuts fold the steps together — pre-adding someone to a [walk list](walk-lists), or simply assigning them a book, both add that person to the team automatically. So if you assign a book to someone new, they join the team on the spot.

> Tip: You don't have to wait for turf to be cut. Pre-add your whole crew to the campaign now, and the books are ready to hand out the moment they're drawn.
