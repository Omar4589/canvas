Lost signal? Keep going. The app is built to work in dead zones.

Every door you record — statuses, surveys, pin fixes — is saved on your phone right away, and the app sends it up automatically the moment you're back online. You don't have to do anything special, and you won't lose a single knock.

If you see a house recolor the instant you tap it but you're offline, that's normal — it's saved locally and will sync later.

## If you change your mind about a door

Change a door's answer and **the most recent one is what sticks** — even if an earlier answer for that same door was still waiting to sync and only goes up afterwards. The app sorts it out by when you actually tapped, not by the order things reach the server, so a late-syncing knock can't undo a correction you made after it.

One thing that **does** need to stay on, even offline: your phone's **location**. GPS comes from satellites, not cell signal — airplane mode or a dead zone won't stop it — but the app records a GPS stamp with every door, so if the Location toggle itself is off, recording is blocked until you turn it back on. Signal off, location on: that's the offline combo that works.

You can tell when work is still waiting to go up: the **Refresh** button (in the bottom-right controls on the map) shows a small **pending badge** with the count of doors that haven't synced yet. It clears on its own once you're back on signal, but you can tap **Refresh** any time to push everything up right away and pull the latest doors.

> Tip: Before heading into an area you know has poor coverage, open your book once while you still have signal so your doors are loaded. After that, you're good to knock offline.
