**Settings** is the last item in the org sidebar, after Billing. Org admins see it; team leads don't. It holds the switches that cover every campaign in your organization. Today that's **Automatic pause**.

## Automatic pause

When automatic pause is on, Doorline pauses a canvasser's recording once their doors come in faster than anyone could knock them, until someone on your team reviews it. What it does, and what to do when it happens, is in [A canvasser was paused](a-canvasser-was-paused).

Only a **billing admin** can change this card. Other org admins see it read-only.

- **On / Off.** Turning it off unpauses anyone who is paused, right away.
- **Who gets the email.** **Billing admins (always)** covers everyone with billing access, including anyone given it later. Under **Also email** are the people you added, each with **Remove**. To add someone, type part of their name under **Add a person** and pick them — any org admin or team lead, up to 25 people.
- **Someone who is no longer an admin or team lead** shows as *Not emailed: no longer an admin or team lead*. Saving drops them.

The email names the campaign and when, never the canvasser. A team lead is only emailed about the campaigns they manage.
