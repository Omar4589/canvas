**Billing** is in the main sidebar. Only admins who've been given billing access can open it — see [Who can see billing?](who-sees-billing).

## Account status

The card at the top shows your account state (Trial, Active, Past due, and so on) and what it means for your team right now. On a trial, it also shows the end date and days remaining.

Below that is a reminder of when the meter starts and stops: a campaign starts billing the month of its first field visit, and keeps billing until you archive it.

## This month

How many campaigns are canvassing this month, with each one listed underneath — the campaign name, the date its billing started, and the doors it's covered so far this month.

Two extra lines appear when they apply:

- **Campaigns in setup** — created but nobody's been out yet. Free until the first knock.
- **Campaigns that started in the last week of the month** — free for this month; billing begins on the 1st.

## Month by month

The history table, newest month first. Each row gives you:

| Column | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Month** | Click it (or the arrow) to open the campaigns behind that month |
| **Billing** | How many campaigns were billing that month |
| **Doors** | The number to invoice from — see *Doors vs knocks* below |
| **Knocks** | Doors a canvasser actually knocked |

Opening a month lists its campaigns with the date each started billing, its doors and knocks, and a short note on any that were free — *not billing yet*, *free — started in the last week*, *free — archived with no knocks*, or *minimum one month*.

It shows 12 months by default. **Show 24 months** extends it.

You won't find prices here. Rates are agreed per client and per race, so what a month cost is a conversation with your account manager rather than a number on a dashboard.

## Your invoicing

**Count restricted homes as billable doors** is your own invoicing policy, not Doorline's.

A restricted home is one your canvasser walked to and couldn't reach — a locked gate, a secured building. Turn this on if you invoice your client per door and want those trips counted. It changes the **Doors** column above, plus your exports and reports. It does **not** change your contact or survey rates, which stay based on doors actually knocked, and it never changes what Doorline charges you.

Individual campaigns can override this from the campaign's edit screen, so one race can count restricted homes while another doesn't.
