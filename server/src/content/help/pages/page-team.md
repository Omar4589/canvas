The **Team** tab is where you add canvassers to the campaign and see the crew. The crew updates automatically as you assign books — assign someone a book and they appear here; unassign them and they drop off. You can also pre-add people before their books are cut.

For the full walkthrough, see [Assigning canvassers](assigning-canvassers).
