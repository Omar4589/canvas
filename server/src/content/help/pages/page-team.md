The **Team** tab is where you fix logins, organize crews and staff a campaign. It lists **this campaign's** people, 25 to a page.

## Finding people

Along the top are quick filters, each with a count: **Current team** (where the page opens), **Never signed in**, **Deactivated** and **Paused**. Under them are a **search box** and filters for **crew** (every crew on this campaign, and **No crew**) and **role**. The counts follow the search and the other filters, so a count always matches the rows it shows.

The columns are **Member**, **Crew**, **Last password login**, **Doors** and **Last door**. Click a column heading to sort, and again to flip the order. Sorting by **Crew** — how the page opens — groups everyone under their coordinator.

**Last password login** is the last time someone typed their password. Doorline keeps people signed in while they use it, so it can read weeks old for someone who works every day. **Never signed in** means the account was made on or after May 15, 2026 and no password has ever been typed. Doorline didn't record sign-ins before that date, so an older account with none on record reads **No sign-in recorded** instead — they may well have signed in — and it's left out of the Never signed in filter and of Resend invites.

## A person's panel

Click anyone to open their panel on the right. The list stays behind it; the arrows at the top step to the next person.

- **The buttons for their state sit at the top**: **Reactivate account** for someone deactivated, **Resend invite** for someone who never signed in, **Unpause** for someone paused, and **Set temporary password** otherwise. Rarer actions are under **More options ▸**.
- **This campaign** shows their crew and their numbers here. Changing the crew shows how many of their doors move before it saves.
- **Finish it**: if an earlier crew change for them didn't finish — say the server restarted while it was saving — or they're on a crew and some of their doors here still count for another team, a line says so with **Finish it**. Like any crew change, it shows the count first.
- **Recent doors** shows their newest 5 on this campaign, with **Show more**.

Opening a person adds them to the page's web address, so you can bookmark or send the link.

## Moving several people to a crew

Tick people (once one is ticked, **Select all** picks everyone the filters show, up to 500), then **Move to crew…**. Before you confirm, you see exactly how many doors move, which client-report teams that changes, and a warning for anyone who runs a crew of their own. The same check one person gets.

Two things can stop a change, and each says so:

- **"Another change for Sam is still finishing — try again in a minute or two."** One change at a time per person.
- **"Their crew changed since you looked."** Someone else changed it in between; check it again before changing it.

If anything goes wrong partway, the people it couldn't change are listed with the reason, and **Retry failed** tries just those people again.

From the same bar, **Resend invites** goes only to people who never signed in, and says how many emails it will send. **Remove from campaign** takes several people off at once.

## Adding people

**Add people** opens one window. **By email** works for everyone: type the address, and Doorline says whether it's a colleague in your organization or a new account, then you pick their **crew** before they're added. Org admins also get **From your organization**: search, tick the people you want, pick one crew for all of them, and add.

**Someone who worked this campaign before** comes back the way crew changes work: their earlier doors here move to the crew they rejoin, or to No team if you pick none. The add step says how many doors they have here and which teams those count for, and picks their old crew for you when that coordinator can still run one. Someone already on the campaign isn't added again, and nothing about them changes; change their crew from their panel. A switched-off person who's already on the campaign gets **Switch on** instead (org admins).

## When someone quits

Open them, then **More options ▸** → **Remove from this campaign**.

**Their work stays — all of it.** Every door they knocked still counts toward the campaign's totals and its bill. That never changes, whatever you do to their account.

**Their books come back.** Any books they held on this campaign are released, so you can hand them to someone else. Books they hold on *other* campaigns aren't touched.

> Looking for someone who left and not finding them? The **Timeline** opens on **today**. Switch the range to **All time** to see everyone who has ever worked this campaign.

If you also want to shut them out of your organization, deactivate their account — that's an org-wide switch you can undo any time. Somebody removed from the campaign keeps their doors on the team they were on until they're added back.

A **team lead** can remove canvassers, people no longer in the organization, and themselves. An org admin removes anyone else.

## Books and the team

A canvasser has to be on the team before you can give them a book. Only org admins and team leads join a team through a book (taking one, or an admin handing them one), and that counts as coming back: their earlier doors here move to No team. Set their crew from their panel afterwards.

## On the phone

In the mobile app the same roster is **More → Users**, with the campaign picked at the top of the list. Its person sheet, filters and "all shown" actions work like this page, except that people are taken off a campaign one at a time there, and its checks don't name client reports. It comes with an app update.
