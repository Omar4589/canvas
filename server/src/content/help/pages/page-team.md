The **Team** tab is where you add canvassers to a campaign and see who's on the crew. Add existing org members, or use **+ New canvasser** to create someone new right onto the team. People also join automatically when you assign them a book or pre-add them to a walk list, so the crew fills in as you cut turf. Click any team member to see their activity here, set their crew, or remove them from the campaign.

Setting someone's **crew** moves the doors they've already knocked *on this campaign* onto it — the app shows you how many before it saves. A crew belongs to this campaign and nothing else: the same canvasser can be on your crew here and on somebody else's in another campaign, and a change you make here doesn't touch their work anywhere else. This tab is the only place a crew is set — the **Users** page just lists, campaign by campaign, whose crew someone is on. See [I moved someone to another team](move-a-canvasser-to-another-team).

## When someone quits

Click them, then **Remove from campaign**. Two things happen, and it's worth being clear about both.

**Their work stays — all of it.** Every door they knocked still counts toward the campaign's totals and its bill, exactly as it did before. That never changes, no matter what you do to their account: removing them, deactivating them, even deleting the account entirely. What they did is a record of something that happened; whether they can still log in is a separate question.

**Their books come back.** Any books they were holding on this campaign are released, so you can hand them to someone else. If other canvassers were also assigned to those books, they keep them — only the person leaving is taken off. Books they hold on *other* campaigns aren't touched.

> Looking for someone who left and not finding them? The **Timeline** opens on **today**, and they haven't knocked today. Switch the range to **All time** to see everyone who has ever worked this campaign, including people who have since left.

If you also want to stop them signing in at all, deactivate their account on the **Users** page — that's an org-wide switch, and you can undo it any time. A deactivated canvasser is still on this campaign's team, so you can still put them on a crew or move them to a different one. Somebody you removed from the campaign isn't — there's no crew left to set. Their doors stay with the team they were on, and if you need to move them later, add them back to the campaign first.

For the full walkthrough, see [Assigning canvassers](assigning-canvassers).
