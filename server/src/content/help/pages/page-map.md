The **Map** page shows all of your campaign's doors, colored by status, so you can see coverage at a glance. Filter by date range to see a specific day's work, and drill into any door for its details. It's the admin's-eye view — the field map on canvassers' phones is scoped to just their assigned doors.

Two filters are worth knowing: **filter to a single canvasser** and the doors recolor to show just **that person's own work** (green only where *they* surveyed, and so on); and **Show overlaps** rings the doors that **more than one canvasser knocked in the same pass** — even across different days. On a campaign with more than one [walk list](walk-lists), an **All walk lists** select also lets you scope the whole map to one list.

For the full walkthrough, see [Using the maps](maps).

## On the phone

Admins and team leads get this map as the **Map** tab in the mobile admin app: every door in the campaign, the canvasser and walk-list filters, the date range, pings, **Show overlaps**, and a door sheet with the same history, voters and answers you get here. It follows the **campaign chip** at the top, which lists **archived** campaigns too — pick one and the map reads read-only: everything is there to look at, and **Move pin** comes off, because a finished campaign's pins stay where the field left them.
