The **Map** page shows all of your campaign's doors, colored by status, so you can see coverage at a glance. Filter by date range to see a specific day's work, and drill into any door for its details. It's the admin's-eye view — the field map on canvassers' phones is scoped to just their assigned doors.

**Apartments don't get their own pins — they get a building.** Every unit is its own door, but they all sit at the same spot, so the map draws one **building icon** for them with a **"N doors"** label. Click it to see and open every door inside. The header counts them (**"39 buildings · 485 stacked doors"** — a pill about what's drawn on screen), which is why the door count can be bigger than the pins you can pick out.

**The big count up top says what it counts.** It reads like **"3,513 doors match · of 10,482 in campaign"**: doors matching your filters across the whole campaign (not just the area on screen), over every door the campaign has on the map — including apartments and doors excluded by **Remove apartments**, which are broken out beside it, because this map is the record of what exists and what was worked, not of what you cut. On **Today**, "match" means doors with a knock or survey today; pick **All time** for every door. If the screen holds fewer than match, a second **"N in view"** says so; click the **ⓘ** for the breakdown. The status chips in the sidebar carry the same campaign-wide count per status, and the survey-answer pills count responses, not doors.

**Doors you left out of your books are still here, and labeled.** The **Layers** panel counts the excluded doors on your screen and lets you **Show**, **Dim**, or **Hide** them for your own view; each one's panel carries a **Not in books** badge. To see only the doors that made it into a cut, scope the map to that pass. Full walkthrough in [Using the maps](maps).

**Marking one door restricted from the desk.** Click a door and its panel has a **Restricted access** section: **Mark restricted…** gives that one home the same slate desk mark a whole-book restrict does (canvassers see it slate, it stays out of every rate and knock count, and the next cut can leave it out) — one confirm, no note. A door marked from the desk reads *Marked from the desk by …* with **Unmark restricted**; a door a canvasser marked restricted at the door reads *Recorded at the door by …* and offers nothing — only a canvasser re-knocking it changes it. The mark lands on the door's walk list's current round (its active round, else the single draft being cut; if there's more than one candidate, the panel asks you to pick). A door still in Intake, or one you've held out of books with **Remove apartments**, can't be marked here. Doors in no book can be marked here in the normal and canvasser views, not in a single-pass view. The Turf Cutting page and the phone have the same action — see [Can I mark just one house restricted?](mark-one-home-restricted).

Two filters are worth knowing: **filter to a single canvasser** and the doors recolor to show just **that person's own work** (green only where *they* surveyed, and so on); and **Show overlaps** rings the doors that **more than one canvasser knocked in the same pass** — even across different days. On a campaign with more than one [walk list](walk-lists), an **All walk lists** select also lets you scope the whole map to one list.

For the full walkthrough, see [Using the maps](maps).

## On the phone

Admins and team leads get this map as the **Map** tab in the mobile admin app: every door in the campaign, the canvasser and walk-list filters, the date range, pings, **Show overlaps**, and a door sheet with the same history, voters and answers you get here. Its count chip reads the same way — **3,513 / 10,482 doors** — tap it for the breakdown; each status in the Status menu carries its campaign-wide count, and the answer menu's numbers are responses, not doors. It follows the **campaign chip** at the top, which lists **archived** campaigns too — pick one and the map reads read-only: everything is there to look at, and **Move pin** and **Mark restricted** come off, because a finished campaign's pins stay where the field left them. On a live campaign the door sheet's **Mark restricted** / **Unmark restricted** row sits just above **Move pin**, labelled with the round it speaks for.
