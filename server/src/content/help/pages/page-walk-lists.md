The **Walk Lists** page is where you create the parallel operations in your campaign, give each one its doors (by claiming a saved search or all Intake), and manage its passes. It also shows an **Intake** count when new imported addresses are waiting to be claimed, plus each walk list's **crew** — which fills in automatically as you assign books.

Each walk list's **Passes** panel shows every pass with its own numbers — books, knocks, survey doors (lit drops on a lit-drop campaign), and connection rate — counted the same way as the campaign Home's **By round** breakdown. The panel is always **all-time**; the Home table follows its date range, so they match exactly when the Home range is "All time".

For the full walkthrough, see [Walk lists](walk-lists) and [Passes](passes).
