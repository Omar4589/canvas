The **Survey** tab is where you attach a survey to the campaign, build or edit its questions, and preview it before canvassers see it. A survey campaign needs a survey attached before you can activate a pass.

Team leads get the full builder here — you can create, edit, and duplicate surveys for a campaign you manage. Archiving or deleting a survey template stays with org admins.

## Preview and Try it

**Preview** shows the survey the way it will read at the door. Statements and closings appear as the words to say, a block that only shows on some paths says when (*Shown when Q1 = "Undecided"*), an answer's read-aloud line sits under it, and canvasser notes and links appear as canvassers will see them. `{{canvasser}}` shows your own first name. Once the survey has closing blocks, the Closing from the bottom of the builder is labelled *when no closing block applies*.

Switch from **Overview** to **Try it** to click through the survey as a canvasser would: one block per screen with **Next**, **‹ Back** and **Skip** if the survey is set up that way, otherwise one page. Pick different answers to walk each path of a script, then **Start over** to try another. Nothing is saved.

For the full walkthrough — including branching questions, scripts with statements, closings and *then go to*, tags, and running different surveys on different walk lists — see [Building and assigning surveys](surveys).
