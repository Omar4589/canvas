A campaign's **Voters** tab (in the Field group of the sidebar, after Map) lists **every voter in that campaign** — one row each, this campaign's own records. Search by **name, Voter ID, or address**; filter by **survey status, voted status, contact status (do not contact or contactable), source ("Added at the door"), or party**. Click a row to open the voter's profile.

Admins and team leads both get this tab. A team lead sees it on the campaigns they manage, and it's the same lookup you already have on the phone under **More → Voter search**.

## The profile

One page with everything about the person: name, Voter ID, phone(s) and email, party, gender, registration and districts; the household and the other people at that address (click to jump); whether they've been marked early-voted ([early voting](page-early-voting)); **this campaign's** survey responses, with answers and notes — if a same-round re-survey replaced someone's answers (a teammate's, or answers an admin typed in at the desk), the earlier response is preserved and shown beneath, and an admin can restore it; answers an admin removed on the Door Outcomes page show as **Removed**, with the undo that would bring them back instead of a Restore button; notes added here plus read-only notes captured in the field (the [Notes page](page-notes) shows them across the whole campaign); and what's happened at the household.

**Editing a survey keeps the earlier answers.** An admin's **Edit** on a survey follows the survey's own logic as you change it, and names any later answer your change would leave no longer asked — that one goes when you save. Saving keeps the earlier version beneath, as an **Earlier answers** card (*Before Ada Admin's change on Oct 19 · recorded by Cara Canvasser*), and the survey reads *Edited ‹date› by ‹admin› · the earlier answers are kept below*. A change to the note is marked on its own line, *Note edited*. If someone changed the survey while you were editing, Save refuses and shows you the latest answers, so you can make your change again. Answers changed with **Edit answers** on the Door Outcomes page land on the profile the same way.

Each earlier-answers card has **Restore these answers…**, which swaps them back (the current answers are kept the same way, so you can swap again), and every kept copy on the profile — a replaced or removed response too — has **Erase…**, which removes that copy for good. Restore works only while the visit still reads Surveyed; otherwise the card says why, and when a Door Outcomes change took the visit, which undo would bring the answers back. **When a voter asks you to remove something they said,** edit the answer, then **Erase** the earlier version — and any other kept card that still holds it, since each kept version is a full copy of the survey, note included. If the card came from a Door Outcomes change, the confirmation says what erasing it means for that change's undo.

If the person is marked **Do not contact**, the profile says so, with the reason, who flagged them, and when — that flag is organization-wide, so it's worth reading before anyone goes back to that door.

**If you're a team lead**, the profile is read-only: you can read everything above and **add a note**, and you can still mark or lift the household's do-not-knock. Changing a voter's details, flagging or clearing do-not-contact, and editing or deleting survey answers stay with admins. You do see what an admin changed, the same way they do: the *Edited* line on a survey and its earlier-answers cards, without Restore or Erase. You also see only this campaign's answers and notes — if the organization has the same person in another campaign, that campaign's records aren't shown here (see [Can two of our campaigns target the same voters?](two-campaigns-same-voters)).

## For admins: the org-wide directory

Admins also have the org-wide **Voters** page at the top level, which shows **every campaign at once** — each person listed once, with a chip for every campaign they're in — plus a campaign dropdown, the do-not-contact list upload, and the do-not-knock addresses. When a campaign is selected there, **Open in campaign →** jumps to that campaign's own tab, and the campaign tab offers **Org-wide directory →** back. Admins keep every edit on a profile, and opening a voter from a campaign still shows the person's other campaigns, with links.

In a large organization the all-campaigns view can occasionally say it **took too long** and offer **Try again** — it's the one view that has to combine every campaign. Picking a campaign there, or using the campaign's own Voters tab, always loads quickly.
