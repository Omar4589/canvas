A campaign's **Voters** tab (in the Field group of the sidebar, after Map) lists **every voter in that campaign** — one row each, this campaign's own records. Search by **name, Voter ID, or address**; filter by **survey status, voted status, contact status (do not contact or contactable), source ("Added at the door"), or party**. Click a row to open the voter's profile.

Admins and team leads both get this tab. A team lead sees it on the campaigns they manage, and it's the same lookup you already have on the phone under **More → Voter search**.

## The profile

One page with everything about the person: name, Voter ID, phone(s) and email, party, gender, registration and districts; the household and the other people at that address (click to jump); whether they've been marked early-voted ([early voting](page-early-voting)); **this campaign's** survey responses, with answers and notes — if a same-round re-survey replaced someone's answers, the earlier response is preserved and shown beneath; notes added here plus read-only notes captured in the field (the [Notes page](page-notes) shows them across the whole campaign); and what's happened at the household.

If the person is marked **Do not contact**, the profile says so, with the reason, who flagged them, and when — that flag is organization-wide, so it's worth reading before anyone goes back to that door.

**If you're a team lead**, the profile is read-only: you can read everything above and **add a note**, and you can still mark or lift the household's do-not-knock. Changing a voter's details, flagging or clearing do-not-contact, and editing or deleting survey answers stay with admins. You also see only this campaign's answers and notes — if the organization has the same person in another campaign, that campaign's records aren't shown here (see [Can two of our campaigns target the same voters?](two-campaigns-same-voters)).

## For admins: the org-wide directory

Admins also have the org-wide **Voters** page at the top level, which shows **every campaign at once** — each person listed once, with a chip for every campaign they're in — plus a campaign dropdown, the do-not-contact list upload, and the do-not-knock addresses. When a campaign is selected there, **Open in campaign →** jumps to that campaign's own tab, and the campaign tab offers **Org-wide directory →** back. Admins keep every edit on a profile, and opening a voter from a campaign still shows the person's other campaigns, with links.

In a large organization the all-campaigns view can occasionally say it **took too long** and offer **Try again** — it's the one view that has to combine every campaign. Picking a campaign there, or using the campaign's own Voters tab, always loads quickly.
