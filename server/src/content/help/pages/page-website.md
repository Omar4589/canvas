**Website** is in the sidebar, under Emails. It has two tabs for the two things the public site at
doorline.app brings in: **Traffic** (how many visits the site gets, and how people got there) and
**Demo requests** (the inbox — the tab shows how many are open).

## Traffic

The page opens here, on **today** (Central time):

- **Visits today**, with how many there were *by this time of day* yesterday and on the same day last
  week. Visits are counted by the hour, so the hour you're in is shared out evenly by the minute.
- **Demo requests today**, and how many are still open — **Open the list** jumps to them.
- **On phones** — the share of today's visitors on a phone, with computers and tablets beside it.
- **Top way in** — how most of today's visitors got here.

Then pick **7, 30 or 90 days**:

- **Visits per day.** Click any day to see it hour by hour. A dot under a bar means a demo request
  came in that day, and a line marks the AAPC event on Nov 17. **Show as a table** gives every number.
- **By the hour** — today, or the day you clicked, with how that day's visitors got here and on what.
  **Back to today** returns to today.
- **How they got here**, in five groups:
  - **QR code & direct** — QR scans, typed or saved addresses, and links in emails and texts. The
    printed AAPC ad's QR code has no tag, so its scans land here; the demo form's *How did you hear
    about us?* tells those leads apart.
  - **Search & AI** — Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, ChatGPT and the like.
  - **Social media** — LinkedIn, Facebook, X and the like.
  - **Other websites** — a link on any other site.
  - **Tagged links** — links you shared with a tag on the end, like `doorline.app/?src=linkedin`.

  Under them, the top sites and tags by name.
- **Where they landed** — the homepage or the AAPC page. Moving from one of our pages to another (say,
  clicking the AAPC banner on the homepage) isn't a new visit, so those moves are listed separately.
  The **canvasser app page** (/app) has its own line too: most people who open it are canvassers a
  customer sent there, not prospects.
- **Phones and computers** for the period. An iPad usually counts as a computer.
- **What it turned into** — the period's demo requests, roughly how many visits it took for each one,
  how people said they heard about us, which page they sent it from, and whether they came through the
  homepage's AAPC banner or a tagged link.

With **Live** on (top right), the tab refreshes itself every minute — handy on a busy day. Press it to
pause.

**How visits are counted.** On our own server, with no cookies and no stored IP addresses or browser
details. Search-engine crawlers, link previews and similar robots are left out, but a reload counts
again, so read the numbers as visits, not people. Visits by the hour, by phone or computer, and to the
canvasser app page are counted from the day this page was updated, so earlier days show a dash for
those in the table.

## Demo requests

Every time someone sends the **Request a demo** form, the request is saved here *and* emailed to
hello@doorline.app. Saving it first means a failed email can no longer lose a lead: if the email
didn't go out, the request still shows here, marked **email failed**.

Each request shows what the person told us — name, organization, work email, what kind of team they
are, team size, how they heard about us, and anything they wrote — plus **Sent from**: the page they
were on, and the link's tag if it had one (for example `src=home-banner` when they came through the
AAPC banner on the homepage; a request from /aapc with no tag says `src=aapc`).

A request from **/aapc** is a booking: *Book 15 minutes with Omar, at the event or after.* It also
shows **When** (at AAPC on Nov 17, or after the event) and, if they gave one, a **Mobile** number —
there's no table at the event, so you text or email them to set the time.

- **Open** is your to-do list. Reply from your inbox (Reply goes straight to them), then press
  **Mark handled** so nobody replies twice. **Reopen** puts it back.
- **Delete** is for when someone asks us to delete what they sent. It's permanent — the only copy
  left is the email in the inbox.
- Requests delete themselves **24 months** after they came in. That's what our privacy policy
  promises.
