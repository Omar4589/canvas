The **Client Reports** tab inside a campaign is where you build the reports your client reads, publish them, fix them later, and manage the links your client opens them from. What your client sees is a separate, read-only page; this tab is where you run it. Org admins and team leads on the campaign can use everything here, except changing the logo, the organization's report week and erasing History text, which are for org admins.

Under the title the page says what it's for: "Make reports for your client here. Each one shows the campaign's running totals and what was added in the dates you pick. Share it with a link and its password, or download a PDF." **How it works** brings back the short guide, **How client reports work**, that sits at the top of the reports list until you press **Got it**:

1. **Make a report** — press New report and pick the dates, usually Last week. After that, Next report on your newest report makes the following one in one click, with your settings and notes copied.
2. **Check it, then publish** — the preview is exactly what your client will see. Once it's published, its numbers stay as they are until you press Refresh numbers.
3. **Share it** — Share gives you a message with the link to paste into your email or text; send the link's password separately. Or open the report and press Download PDF.

**The reports.** Reports are grouped into the same tabs your client sees: **Whole campaign**, each team, and each walk list, newest first. A line between two reports marks any days no report covers. Each row shows the period, whether it's a **Draft**, **Published** or **Taken down**, when it was edited or refreshed, its headline numbers, and how often your client opened it ("Opened via Candidate: about 2 visits"). While its numbers are building, the row shows **Queued**, **Waiting** or **Building** with a percentage, and **Cancel**; a build that stopped shows **Retry**. The row's buttons are **Open**, **Next report** (on the newest report of each tab, and on its newest Latest progress report), **Share** (on every report your client can see: the message to send, the link it uses, and a way to make a link to all your reports or to just this one), and a menu with **Take down**, **Put back up** and **Delete**. A team's tab has **Edit team**, or **Continue this team** once the team has ended; a walk list's tab has **Rename**.

**New report** starts a draft for the whole campaign, a team, or one walk list. Its dates follow your organization's report week (Monday to Sunday unless an admin changes it, right there in the dialog): first what comes after the tab's last report, then **Latest progress · all time, through today**, **Last week**, **This week, not over yet** and **Pick dates**. After a long gap it suggests the most recent full week or month and names the days no report covers. A line above the dates says what they decide: the totals run from the campaign's first door through the last day you pick, and the first day only decides what shows as new ("+412 this week"). **Latest progress · all time, through today** makes a report of everything so far, with no "+" figures (or leave Pick dates' first day empty for everything up to a day); see [Client reports and share links](client-reports). If the tab already has a report for those dates, it asks before making a second one. Its numbers build in the background, so you can leave the page.

**Opening a report** shows the builder: your settings on the left and, on the right, a preview of exactly what your client will see. A draft saves itself as you go, lists what's worth a look under **Before you publish** (on a team report, each team's door visits this period beside the whole campaign's), and goes live with **Publish…**, which freezes exactly what your screen shows. A published report has **Edit**, **Refresh numbers**, **Share** and **Take down** (or **Put back up**), and a **Worth a look** box when a check still applies (a map question with no answers by area, answers from an older survey version, an earlier report refreshed since). **Refresh numbers** on a report whose map is off offers its earlier map questions already ticked.

**If someone else has the same report open**, neither of you overwrites the other: a save made against an older version is merged with theirs, so your screen updates, or keeps both sides' changes (lists of charts, map questions and voter groups merge one by one), and asks **Keep mine** or **Use theirs** only when you both changed the same thing, saying what Keep mine replaces. If they publish a draft you're editing, your change waits as an unsent edit for you to review and send. A change that didn't save before you left comes back as **Restore it** or **discard it**; an unsent edit of a published report comes back as **Restore your unsent edit**, and **Edit** opens it too.

**Client links**, beside the reports, holds the campaign's links: each one's label, what it opens (all your reports, or one report), address, expiry, when it was last opened, and any wrong passwords, with **Extend** or **Renew**, **Change password**, **Rotate**, **Unlock now** when it's locked, and a menu to disable or delete it. Links to one report fold under **Links to one report**, and say so while their report is taken down. **+ New link** makes a link to all your reports or to one report, with a password shown once. Deleting a report deletes its one-report links.

**Report branding** shows your organization's logo on client reports. Org admins can upload, change or remove it; team leads see it.

**History** opens the tab's own history: every publish, edit, refresh, take-down, delete, team change and link change, with who did it and when, each row naming its report's tab or its team. It doesn't appear in the campaign's general History or on the phone.

For the full walkthrough, see [Client reports and share links](client-reports).
