The **Client Reports** page is where you build a report for a client, publish it (which freezes the numbers and snapshots the map), and share it through a public link that needs no login. What your client opens is a separate, read-only public page — this tab is where you build and manage it.

For the full walkthrough, see [Client reports and share links](client-reports).
