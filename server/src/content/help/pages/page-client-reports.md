The **Client Reports** tab inside a campaign is where you build the reports your client reads, publish them, fix them later, and manage the links your client opens them from. What your client sees is a separate, read-only page; this tab is where you run it. Org admins and team leads on the campaign can use everything here, except changing the logo and erasing History text, which are for org admins.

**The reports.** Reports are grouped into the same tabs your client sees: **Whole campaign**, each team, and each walk list, newest first. A line between two reports marks any days no report covers. Each row shows the period, whether it's a **Draft**, **Published** or **Taken down**, when it was edited or refreshed, its headline numbers, and how often your client opened it ("Opened via Candidate: about 2 visits"). While its numbers are building, the row shows **Queued**, **Waiting** or **Building** with a percentage, and **Cancel**; a build that stopped shows **Retry**. The row's buttons are **Open**, **Next report** (on the newest report of each tab), **Copy message** (on a report your client can see, when a link can open it), and a menu with **Take down**, **Put back up** and **Delete**. A team's tab has **Edit team**, or **Continue this team** once the team has ended; a walk list's tab has **Rename**.

**New report** starts a draft for the whole campaign, a team, or one walk list, for any run of days. It suggests the days after your last report of the same kind; after a long gap it suggests the same length ending yesterday and names the days no report covers. Its numbers build in the background, so you can leave the page.

**Opening a report** shows the builder: your settings on the left and, on the right, a preview of exactly what your client will see. A draft saves itself as you go, lists what's worth a look under **Before you publish** (on a team report, each team's door visits this period beside the whole campaign's), and goes live with **Publish…**, which freezes exactly what your screen shows. A published report has **Edit**, **Refresh numbers**, **Copy message** and **Take down** (or **Put back up**), and a **Worth a look** box when a check still applies (a map question with no answers by area, answers from an older survey version, an earlier report refreshed since). **Refresh numbers** on a report whose map is off offers its earlier map questions already ticked.

**If someone else has the same report open**, neither of you overwrites the other: a save made against an older version is merged with theirs, so your screen updates, or keeps both sides' changes (lists of charts, map questions and voter groups merge one by one), and asks **Keep mine** or **Use theirs** only when you both changed the same thing, saying what Keep mine replaces. If they publish a draft you're editing, your change waits as an unsent edit for you to review and send. A change that didn't save before you left comes back as **Restore it** or **discard it**; an unsent edit of a published report comes back as **Restore your unsent edit**, and **Edit** opens it too.

**Client links**, beside the reports, holds the campaign's links: each one's label, address, expiry, when it was last opened, and any wrong passwords, with **Extend** or **Renew**, **Change password**, **Rotate**, **Unlock now** when it's locked, and a menu to disable or delete it. **+ New link** makes a link with a password shown once.

**Report branding** shows your organization's logo on client reports. Org admins can upload, change or remove it; team leads see it.

**History** opens the tab's own history: every publish, edit, refresh, take-down, delete, team change and link change, with who did it and when, each row naming its report's tab or its team. It doesn't appear in the campaign's general History or on the phone.
