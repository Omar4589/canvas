The **Survey Explorer** turns any survey count into the people behind it. Pick a question and an answer, and you get every matching entry — the voter, the address, **which canvasser recorded it and exactly when**, any note they typed, and a map of exactly those doors.

## Pick a drill

Use the filters across the top: the question, the answer (chips with live counts), a canvasser, a walk list, and a date range. The page opens on **today's** entries — widen the range whenever you want the full picture. The whole drill lives in the page's address, so you can bookmark a view or send the link to another admin.

## Voters and By canvasser

Two views of the same answer:

- **Voters** lists every entry — click one to open the full response (all its answers, the note, when it was submitted and synced, and whether an admin later edited it).
- **By canvasser** ranks who recorded the answer: how many, what share of the answer that is, and what share of *their own* answers to the question. Click a canvasser to filter the whole page to just their entries; click again to clear.

## Map and export

A mini map shows exactly the filtered doors, and **Open in Map** takes you to the full Map page with the same filters already set. **Export CSV** downloads the current drill as a spreadsheet.

> Tip: For a quick answer to "who wants a yard sign today?" — pick that question, the **Yes** chip, leave the range on Today, and export or open the map. That's your pickup list.

You can also get here from the campaign Home: expand any answer in the survey results and click **Open full view**.
