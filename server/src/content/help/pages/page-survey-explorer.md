The **Survey Explorer** turns any survey count into the people behind it. Pick a question and an answer, and you get every matching entry — the voter, the address, **which canvasser recorded it and exactly when**, the answer itself, any note they typed, and a map of exactly those doors.

If a question uses **[Other (specify)](surveys)**, the write-ins get their own **Other** answer to pick, and each row shows what that person typed — so "what did people actually say?" is a drill, not an export you have to open in a spreadsheet.

## Pick a drill

Use the filters across the top: the question, the answer (chips with live counts), a canvasser, a walk list, a **coordinator crew** (once your campaign has coordinators — the same filter the campaign Home and Timeline have), and a date range. The page opens on **today's** entries — widen the range whenever you want the full picture. The whole drill lives in the page's address, so you can bookmark a view or send the link to another admin. Opening the Explorer from a crew-filtered dashboard drill keeps that crew selected, so the counts here match the ones you clicked. One heads-up: the map shows the matching doors campaign-wide — doors don't belong to a crew, so the crew filter narrows the lists, not the pins.

## Tags

Before you pick a question, the page shows the survey's **Tags** panel — each tag with its **voters identified** (people, once each) and how many are **still current**. Click a tag to drill it: you get two headline cards (identified / still current), a **By team** table (the team that found each voter first gets the credit, so the rows add up exactly), the entries list, and the CSV. Tag drills have no By-canvasser view — several questions can feed one voter's tag, so "who recorded it" has no single answer — and no map, since the map filters by a single question's answer. See [How do I count our supporters?](how-do-i-count-supporters).

## Voters and By canvasser

Two views of the same answer:

- **Voters** lists every entry — click one to open the full response (all its answers, the note, when it was submitted and synced, and whether an admin later edited it).
- **By canvasser** ranks who recorded the answer: how many, what share of the answer that is, and what share of *their own* answers to the question. Click a canvasser to filter the whole page to just their entries; click again to clear.

## Map and export

The map sits above the list and shows exactly the filtered doors. **Click any pin** for that
door's details — who lives there, who recorded the answer and when, and their note. Click a
person in that card to open their full response, the same view the list below opens. A pin
covering an apartment building lists every matching unit at that address.

Use the **⤢** button in the map's header to fill the screen with it; **Esc** closes the door
card, and **Esc** again leaves fullscreen.

**Export CSV** downloads the current drill as a spreadsheet.

When a drill turns out to be a cleanup job — a run of answers that shouldn't be there — **Correct in Door Outcomes** (org admins, beside Export CSV on an answer drill) opens the [Door Outcomes page](page-door-outcomes) with this drill's question, answer and filters already applied.

> Tip: For a quick answer to "who wants a yard sign today?" — pick that question, the **Yes**
> chip, leave the range on Today, and read the map or export. That's your pickup list.

You can also get here from the campaign Home: expand any answer in the survey results and click **Open full view**.
