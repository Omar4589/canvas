The **Notes** page pulls every note left across the campaign into one read-only feed, from three sources: **door** notes a canvasser types when marking a house, **survey** notes left when submitting a response, and **admin** notes kept on a voter's profile. It opens on today's notes, and you can filter by date, type, author, or walk list, or search the text.

Tap any note to jump to its context — a note about a voter opens that voter's profile, and a door-only note opens the map zoomed to that door. It's view-only here: you edit an admin note on the voter's profile, and door and survey notes are the canvasser's own record. (Door notes also still show on the household's map panel.)
