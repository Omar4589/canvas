The **Notes** page pulls every note left across the campaign into one read-only feed, from three sources: **door** notes a canvasser types when marking a house, **survey** notes left when submitting a response, and **admin** notes kept on a voter's profile. It opens on today's notes, and you can filter by date, type, **door outcome**, author, or walk list, or search the text. Filtering by outcome narrows the door notes to the ones left on a Not home, Refused, Lit dropped and so on; admin notes have no door outcome, so they step aside while an outcome is selected.

**Export these** hands whatever you are looking at to the Export Center as a **Notes** file — one row per note, with who wrote it and the door or voter it belongs to. Two things worth knowing: this screen shows at most 500 notes per source but the file is not capped, and a door note names nobody on its own, so listing the people registered at that door is an option you tick rather than the default.

Tap any note to jump to its context — a note about a voter opens that voter's profile, and a door-only note opens the map zoomed to that door. It's view-only here: you edit an admin note on the voter's profile, and door and survey notes are the canvasser's own record. (Door notes also still show on the household's map panel.)

## On the phone

Admins and team leads get the same feed in the app, two ways: **More → Notes**, or the **Notes** tile on a campaign's own page. Same three sources, same filters, same tap-through. Because a phone has no address bar to hold the campaign, the screen carries a **campaign chip** at the top — tap it to switch, and your pick carries across your other admin screens. Opening Notes from a campaign's own tile scopes it to that campaign.

The chip lists **archived** campaigns too, under an **Archived · read-only** divider, so a finished campaign's notes stay readable from your phone. You'll see an **Archived — read-only** note at the top, which changes nothing here — the Notes feed never let you edit anything in the first place.
