# Data Processing Addendum

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") is entered into between **Doorline LLC**, a Texas limited liability company ("Doorline"), and the customer identified in the signature block below ("Customer"), and is incorporated into and forms part of the Terms of Service and any ordering agreement, proposal, or invoice between the parties (together, the "Agreement"). If this DPA conflicts with the Agreement on the subject of Personal Information processing, this DPA controls.

## 1. Definitions

**"Customer Data"** has the meaning given in the Agreement: the voter files and other data Customer uploads to or generates in the Service, including canvassing records created through Customer's use of the Service.

**"Personal Information"** means information within Customer Data that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked with a particular individual or household, as defined under Applicable Data Protection Laws.

**"Applicable Data Protection Laws"** means the U.S. federal and state privacy and data-protection laws that apply to the processing of Personal Information under the Agreement, including, as applicable, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA"), the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act, the Colorado Privacy Act, the Connecticut Data Privacy Act, and other similar state laws as they take effect.

**"Sell," "Share," "Service Provider," "Processor," "Controller," "Business," "Business Purpose,"** and **"Consumer"** have the meanings given in Applicable Data Protection Laws.

## 2. Roles and scope

Customer is the Business or Controller of Personal Information in Customer Data. Doorline processes that Personal Information as Customer's Service Provider and Processor, solely to provide the Service described in the Agreement. Customer's documented instructions to Doorline consist of the Agreement, this DPA, and Customer's use and configuration of the Service's features. Customer represents that it has provided all notices and obtained all rights and consents required under Applicable Data Protection Laws to permit the processing described here, as further set out in Section 3 of the Terms of Service.

## 3. Doorline's obligations

Doorline shall:

(a) process Personal Information only to provide the Service to Customer and for the Business Purposes described in the Agreement and Doorline's Privacy Policy, and not for any other purpose;

(b) **not Sell or Share Personal Information**;

(c) not retain, use, or disclose Personal Information for any purpose other than performing the Service, or outside the direct business relationship between Doorline and Customer, except as permitted by Applicable Data Protection Laws;

(d) not combine Personal Information received from Customer with personal information received from another customer or collected from any other source, except as permitted by Applicable Data Protection Laws for the Business Purposes of providing the Service;

(e) ensure that each person Doorline authorizes to process Personal Information is subject to a duty of confidentiality;

(f) notify Customer without undue delay if Doorline determines that it can no longer meet its obligations under Applicable Data Protection Laws or this DPA, in which case Customer may take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and remediate unauthorized use of Personal Information;

(g) permit Customer to take reasonable and appropriate steps to ensure that Doorline uses Personal Information consistently with Customer's obligations under Applicable Data Protection Laws, including by making available the information described in Section 8.

**Doorline certifies that it understands the restrictions in this Section 3 and will comply with them.**

## 4. Security

Doorline shall maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect Personal Information against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure, appropriate to the nature of the data. These currently include encryption of data in transit, storage on infrastructure that encrypts data at rest, tenant isolation limiting each customer's users to that customer's data, time-limited and reason-logged controls on Doorline staff access to Customer Data, and audit logging of that access, as described in Doorline's Privacy Policy. Doorline shall not materially decrease the overall protection of Personal Information during the term of the Agreement.

## 5. Security incidents

Doorline shall notify Customer without undue delay after confirming a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, or unauthorized disclosure of or access to Personal Information, and shall provide information reasonably available to Doorline to assist Customer in meeting Customer's own notification obligations.

## 6. Subprocessors

Customer provides general authorization for Doorline to engage subprocessors to provide the Service. Doorline's current subprocessors are: Heroku (application hosting, United States); MongoDB Atlas (database hosting, United States); Mapbox (map display); Geocodio (address geocoding); Resend (transactional email delivery, United States); Expo (mobile application builds and software updates); and Apple and Google (mobile application distribution). Doorline shall (a) impose on each subprocessor data-protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA, to the extent applicable to the services the subprocessor performs; (b) remain responsible for each subprocessor's performance; and (c) give Customer notice (which may be by posting or through the Service) before adding or replacing a subprocessor that processes Personal Information. If Customer reasonably objects to a change on data-protection grounds and the parties cannot resolve the objection, Customer may terminate the Agreement as to the affected Service.

## 7. Assistance with consumer requests

Taking into account the nature of the processing, Doorline shall provide reasonable assistance to Customer in responding to verifiable Consumer requests to exercise rights under Applicable Data Protection Laws (such as access, correction, and deletion), including through the Service's export and deletion capabilities. If Doorline receives such a request directly and can identify the controlling Customer, Doorline will refer the request to Customer and will not respond substantively except as required by law.

## 8. Demonstrating compliance

Upon Customer's reasonable written request, no more than once per year (except following a security incident), Doorline shall make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate its compliance with this DPA, in the form of written responses to reasonable inquiries and summaries of Doorline's relevant policies and controls. This Section does not require Doorline to provide access to other customers' data or to information that would compromise the security of the Service.

## 9. Retention, return, and deletion

Doorline retains and deletes Customer Data as described in the Agreement and Doorline's Privacy Policy, which are Customer's documented instructions for retention: upon termination, Customer retains read-only access for 60 days to export Customer Data, after which Doorline will delete it; suspended accounts with no canvassing activity for approximately 30 months are deleted; and Customer may request deletion at any time, which Doorline aims to complete within 30 days. As a further documented instruction, when an individual user deletes their account, Doorline retains the user's name in association with that user's canvassing records for 180 days by default so that Customer can attribute field work for fraud- and quality-prevention purposes, after which the name is removed. Doorline may retain (i) records documenting deletions and staff access, (ii) records required for legal, billing, or dispute-resolution purposes, and (iii) aggregate, non-identifying usage statistics that contain no Personal Information. Residual copies in encrypted backups are overwritten in the ordinary rotation of those backups and are used only for disaster recovery.

## 10. Term

This DPA takes effect when executed and continues for as long as Doorline processes Personal Information under the Agreement. Sections 3, 5, and 9 survive until Doorline no longer holds Personal Information.

## 11. General

This DPA is governed by the law governing the Agreement. It may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature or by exchange of signed PDF copies, each of which is deemed an original.
